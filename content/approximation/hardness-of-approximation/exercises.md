1. ◦ Turn each [gap problem](/gap-problem/) into a bound on the ratio. (a) For a maximization problem it is NP-hard to tell instances with $\mathrm{OPT} \ge 0.96\,m$ from those with $\mathrm{OPT} < 0.9\,m$. (b) For a minimization problem it is NP-hard to tell $\mathrm{OPT} \le 4$ from $\mathrm{OPT} \ge 5$. (c) A minimization problem has integer optima, and it is NP-hard to tell $\mathrm{OPT} \le 3$ from $\mathrm{OPT} \ge 4$. Show that this rules out every ratio below $4/3$ but not an algorithm that always returns at most $\mathrm{OPT} + 1$, and check that the [chromatic index](/chromatic-index/) of graphs has both properties.

2. ◦ Run the reduction of [general TSP inapproximability](/general-tsp-inapproximability/) with $\rho = 2$ on the complete bipartite graph $K_{2,3}$. Give the costs, find OPT and say what the decision procedure answers. Then build the instance with costs 1 and 2 used in [metric TSP inapproximability](/metric-tsp-inapproximability/) for the same graph, and find its OPT.

3. ◦ Apply the reduction from partition in [bin packing inapproximability](/bin-packing-inapproximability/), which divides each number by half the total, to the sets $\{3, 5, 7, 9\}$ and $\{2, 4, 6, 9, 11\}$. Find the optimal number of bins in each case, and say how many bins an algorithm with ratio below $3/2$ must use on each.

4. ◦ The reduction in [k-center inapproximability](/k-center-inapproximability/) turns the cycle $C_6$, with vertices $0, \dots, 5$, into a metric with distance 1 between neighbours on the cycle and 2 between all other pairs. Find the optimal radius for $k = 1$ and $k = 2$. Then run [farthest-first traversal](/gonzalez-k-center/) from vertex 0 with $k = 2$, and find which tie-breaking choices give ratio exactly 2.

5. ◦ The [L-reduction](/l-reduction/) page maps vertex cover in graphs of degree at most 3 with no isolated vertex to independent set, with constants $a = 3$ and $b = 1$. An algorithm always finds an independent set of size at least $0.9\,\alpha(G)$. What ratio for vertex cover does the reduction give? For which ratios $r$ of the independent set algorithm, in place of $0.9$, does the result beat the ratio 2 of the [maximal matching algorithm](/vertex-cover-2-approximation/)?

6. ◦ Adapt the clause-variable game of the [two-prover one-round game](/two-prover-one-round-game/) page to formulas with two literals per clause: the verifier picks a clause and one of its two variables. Find the value of the game for the four clauses $(x \lor y)$, $(x \lor \lnot y)$, $(\lnot x \lor y)$, $(\lnot x \lor \lnot y)$, and compare it with the largest fraction of these clauses that one assignment satisfies.

7. ◦ A [unique game](/unique-games-conjecture/) over $\mathbb{Z}_5$ has variables $x_1, \dots, x_4$ and the constraints $x_1 - x_2 = 1$, $x_2 - x_3 = 2$, $x_3 - x_1 = 2$, $x_3 - x_4 = 4$ and $x_4 - x_1 = 3$. Find a satisfying assignment. Then change the last constraint to $x_4 - x_1 = 1$ and find the value of the new game.

8. ◦ Run the max cut [dictatorship test](/dictatorship-test/) with $\rho = -1/3$. Compute the acceptance probability of a dictator, of $\operatorname{Maj}_3$, and the limit for $\operatorname{Maj}_n$ as $n$ grows. Compare the ratio of the last to the first with $\alpha_{\mathrm{GW}} \approx 0.87856$.

9. ◦ A $k$-median instance in the style of [k-median inapproximability](/k-median-inapproximability/) has clients $1, \dots, 7$ and five possible facilities, the sets $S_1 = \{1, 2, 3\}$, $S_2 = \{3, 4, 5\}$, $S_3 = \{5, 6, 7\}$, $S_4 = \{1, 4, 7\}$ and $S_5 = \{2, 6\}$. A client is at distance 1 from each set that contains it and 3 from the others, and two clients, or two facilities, are at distance 2. Check that this is a metric, find the optimal cost for $k = 2$, and compare it with the cost if two of the sets covered every client.

10. • The cost 1 and 2 instance of [metric TSP inapproximability](/metric-tsp-inapproximability/) has $\mathrm{OPT} = n$ when the graph is Hamiltonian and $\mathrm{OPT} \ge n + 1$ otherwise. Show that this rules out an FPTAS for the metric TSP unless P = NP, but says nothing against a PTAS. What would a gap of $\mathrm{OPT} = n$ against $\mathrm{OPT} \ge (1 + c)\,n$ give?

11. • In the reduction of [Steiner tree inapproximability](/steiner-tree-inapproximability/), the terminals are the edges of a graph $G$, the Steiner points are its vertices, two vertices are at distance 1, a vertex and an edge are at distance 1 if the vertex is an end of the edge and 2 otherwise, and two edges are at distance 2. Find the optimal tree for $G = C_5$ and for $G = K_4$, check the formula $\mathrm{OPT} = m + \tau(G) - 1$ on both, and check the first L-reduction condition with the constant $\Delta + 1$.

12. • Starting from the hardness of MAX E3SAT within $7/8 + \varepsilon$ on satisfiable instances, show that for each $k \ge 3$ it is NP-hard to approximate MAX E$k$SAT, with exactly $k$ distinct variables per clause, within $1 - 2^{-k} + \varepsilon$.

13. • For a clause $a \lor b \lor c$ and a fresh variable $d$, consider the ten clauses $(a)$, $(b)$, $(c)$, $(d)$, $(\lnot a \lor \lnot b)$, $(\lnot a \lor \lnot c)$, $(\lnot b \lor \lnot c)$, $(a \lor \lnot d)$, $(b \lor \lnot d)$, $(c \lor \lnot d)$. Show that the best choice of $d$ satisfies 7 of them when $a \lor b \lor c$ is true and 6 when it is false. Deduce from [MAX 3SAT inapproximability](/max-3sat-inapproximability/) that [MAX 2SAT](/max-2-sat/) has no ratio above $55/56 + \varepsilon$ unless P = NP.

14. • A verifier checks a 2-colouring of the triangle on vertices 1, 2, 3. The proof is three bits, one colour per vertex. Two random bits choose an edge, $00 \mapsto \{1, 2\}$, $01 \mapsto \{2, 3\}$, $10 \mapsto \{1, 3\}$, $11 \mapsto \{1, 2\}$, and the verifier accepts when the two colours differ. Build its FGLSS graph as in [clique inapproximability](/clique-inapproximability/), count its vertices and edges, and check that its clique number is the largest number of random strings that one proof makes the verifier accept.

15. • The join of graphs $G_1, \dots, G_k$ is their disjoint union with every edge added between different $G_i$. Show that the join of $k$ copies of $G$ has chromatic number $k\,\chi(G)$. Use this to show that for each $\varepsilon > 0$ and each constant $d$, no polynomial algorithm colours every graph with at most $(4/3 - \varepsilon)\chi(G) + d$ colours unless P = NP, a weak form of a theorem cited in [graph colouring inapproximability](/graph-coloring-inapproximability/).

16. • A reduction maps each 3SAT formula of size $N$, in time $N^{O(\log \log N)}$, to a set cover instance with $n \le N^{O(\log \log N)}$ elements, such that satisfiable formulas give covers of size at most $t$ and unsatisfiable ones need more than $c\,t \ln n$ sets. Show that a polynomial algorithm with ratio $c \ln n$ for set cover gives NP $\subseteq$ DTIME$(N^{O(\log \log N)})$, and explain why this does not give P = NP. Compare with the two theorems in [set cover inapproximability](/set-cover-inapproximability/).

17. • Let $f : \{-1, 1\}^3 \to \{-1, 1\}$ be the multiplexer: $f(x) = x_1$ when $x_3 = 1$ and $f(x) = x_2$ when $x_3 = -1$. Compute its [Fourier expansion](/fourier-analysis-of-boolean-functions/), the acceptance probability of the [linearity test](/linearity-test/), the distance from $f$ to the nearest parity, and the influence of each variable.

18. • Show that a [unique game](/unique-games-conjecture/) with label set $\{0, 1\}$ is an instance of Max 2LIN over $\mathbb{Z}_2$. Use the Goemans-Williamson algorithm to show that for $\varepsilon < 0.31$ the problem of telling value at least $1 - \varepsilon$ from value at most $0.6$ is in P on such games. Why must the conjecture let the number of labels grow as the two values move towards 1 and 0?

19. • Place the vertices $j = 0, \dots, 6$ of the cycle $C_7$ at the unit vectors with angle $6\pi j/7$. Compute the value of the max cut [semidefinite program](/semidefinite-programming/) at these vectors and the size of the maximum cut, and bound the [integrality gap](/integrality-gap/) on $C_7$. Do the same for $C_{2m+1}$, and find the limit of the ratio as $m$ grows. Compare with the 5-cycle on the [max cut UGC hardness](/max-cut-ugc-hardness/) page.

20. • For each 3-bit predicate, find its density, decide whether its accepting set supports a balanced pairwise independent distribution, and decide whether it is [approximation resistant](/approximation-resistance/): (a) majority, $\operatorname{MAJ}_3(x) = 1$ when at least two bits are 1; (b) $P(x) = x_1 \lor (x_2 \oplus x_3)$.

21. ★ In the $k$-supplier problem the points are split into clients and suppliers, and $k$ centres must be chosen among the suppliers to minimize the largest distance from a client to its nearest centre. Show that ratio $3 - \varepsilon$ is NP-hard, by a reduction from set cover in the style of [k-center inapproximability](/k-center-inapproximability/).

22. ★ Show that [dominating set](/dominating-set-inapproximability/) has no PTAS on graphs of bounded degree unless P = NP. Use an L-reduction that adds one new vertex for each edge, from vertex cover in graphs of maximum degree $B$, which is [APX-complete](/apx-completeness/) for a suitable constant $B$. What degree bound do you get?

23. ★ The lexicographic product $G[H]$ has vertex set $V(G) \times V(H)$, and $(u, v)$ is adjacent to $(u', v')$ when $uu' \in E(G)$, or $u = u'$ and $vv' \in E(H)$. Show that $\alpha(G[H]) = \alpha(G)\,\alpha(H)$, and that from an independent set $S$ of $G[H]$ one can find in polynomial time an independent set of $G$ or of $H$ of size at least $\sqrt{|S|}$. Deduce that if [maximum independent set](/maximum-independent-set/) has a polynomial algorithm with some constant ratio $\alpha > 0$, it has a PTAS, and conclude that it has no constant ratio unless P = NP.

24. ★ In the CHSH game of the [two-prover one-round game](/two-prover-one-round-game/) page, the questions $x, y$ are uniform bits, the answers are bits $a, b$, and the verifier accepts when $a \oplus b = x \land y$; its value is $3/4$. Find the value of the game played twice in parallel, where the verifier accepts only when both coordinates are accepted. Compare it with $3/4$ and $(3/4)^2$, and say what this shows about the [parallel repetition theorem](/parallel-repetition-theorem/).

25. ★ Show that the max cut [semidefinite program](/semidefinite-programming/) of a graph with $n$ vertices has value at most $\frac{n}{4}\lambda_{\max}(L)$, where $L$ is the [Laplacian matrix](/laplacian-matrix/). Use it to find the exact value of the program on the odd cycle $C_n$ and the exact integrality gap on $C_7$.
