## History

The oldest result of the chapter is about polyhedra, not meshes. Descartes noted, in a manuscript of about 1630 that survives through a copy by Leibniz, that the angle defects of a convex polyhedron add up to eight right angles. Euler published $V - E + F = 2$ in 1758, Banchoff in 1970 treated the angle defect as curvature, and Tutte's 1963 paper "How to draw a graph" gave the drawing that later became the first mesh parameterization.

Mesh processing grew in the 1990s, when scanners and simplification produced large meshes. Pinkall and Polthier derived the cotangent formula in 1993. Taubin in 1995 viewed smoothing as filtering on the mesh graph and also estimated the curvature tensor from edge samples, and Desbrun, Meyer, Schröder and Barr made smoothing implicit in 1999. Meyer, Desbrun, Schröder and Barr gave the mean curvature normal with mixed areas in 2003, the year Cohen-Steiner and Morvan built a tensor from normal cycles; Vallet and Lévy computed manifold harmonics in 2008. Hoppe introduced progressive meshes in 1996, Rossignac Edgebreaker in 1999 and, with Safonova and Szymczak, the corner table in 2001. Möller published his triangle test in 1997, and Cignoni, Rocchini and Scopigno released Metro in 1998.

Mitchell, Mount and Papadimitriou gave exact geodesics in 1987, Chen and Han improved the bounds in 1990, and Crane, Weischedel and Wardetzky replaced propagation by two solves in the heat method of 2013. Floater used Tutte's system for surfaces in 1997 and gave mean value coordinates in 2003; Sheffer and de Sturler introduced angle-based flattening in 2001, and Lévy, Petitjean, Ray and Maillot least squares conformal maps in 2002. Erickson and Har-Peled showed in 2004 that the shortest cut graph is NP-hard. Sorkine and coauthors edited surfaces by Laplacian coordinates in 2004, and Sorkine and Alexa made the local and global ARAP steps in 2007, which Liu and coauthors applied to parameterization in 2008. Springborn, Schröder and Pinkall made discrete conformal maps a convex problem in 2008, and Sawhney and Crane gave boundary first flattening in 2017.

Repair became a subject of its own with 3D printing and scanning. Barequet and Sharir filled gaps with the triangulation dynamic program in 1995, Barequet, Dickerson and Eppstein showed in 1998 that a patch that does not cross itself is NP-complete to find, Liepa added fairing in 2003, and Ju gave volumetric repair in 2004. Jacobson, Kavan and Sorkine-Hornung defined generalized winding numbers in 2013, and Zhou, Grinspun, Zorin and Jacobson used them with exact arrangements in 2016.

For remeshing and segmentation, Botsch and Kobbelt reduced isotropic remeshing to four local steps in 2004, the year Cohen-Steiner, Alliez and Desbrun gave variational shape approximation. Shapira, Shamir and Cohen-Or defined the shape diameter function in 2008, when Ray, Vallet, Li and Lévy also defined the index of symmetric direction fields. Bommes, Zimmer and Kobbelt posed quad meshing as a mixed-integer problem in 2009, and Knöppel, Crane, Pinkall and Schröder found globally optimal fields of the relaxed problem in 2013.

## Further reading

- Mario Botsch, Leif Kobbelt, Mark Pauly, Pierre Alliez and Bruno Lévy, *Polygon Mesh Processing* (A K Peters, 2010). The standard textbook for the whole chapter, from data structures, smoothing and curvature to deformation, parameterization, remeshing and repair.
- Marco Attene, Marcel Campen and Leif Kobbelt, "Polygon mesh repairing: an application perspective", *ACM Computing Surveys* 45(2), 2013. Defects and repair methods sorted by the use of the result, local and volumetric side by side.
- Michael S. Floater and Kai Hormann, "Surface parameterization: a tutorial and survey", in *Advances in Multiresolution for Geometric Modelling* (Springer, 2005). The theory behind Tutte maps, mean value coordinates and conformal maps, with the proofs of injectivity.
- Alla Sheffer, Emil Praun and Kenneth Rose, "Mesh parameterization methods and their applications", *Foundations and Trends in Computer Graphics and Vision* 2(2), 2006. A wider survey, including cutting and distortion measures.
- Ariel Shamir, "A survey on mesh segmentation techniques", *Computer Graphics Forum* 27(6), 2008. Segmentation into surface patches and into parts, with the algorithms of each.
- David Bommes and coauthors, "Quad-mesh generation and processing: a survey", *Computer Graphics Forum* 32(6), 2013. Cross fields, global parameterization and quad extraction, the context of mixed-integer quadrangulation.
