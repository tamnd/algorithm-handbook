## History

The oldest result of the chapter is about polyhedra, not meshes. Descartes noted, in a manuscript of about 1630 that survives through a copy by Leibniz, that the angle defects of a convex polyhedron add up to eight right angles. Euler published $V - E + F = 2$ in 1758, and Banchoff in 1970 treated the angle defect as the curvature of a polyhedral surface. Tutte's 1963 paper "How to draw a graph" gave the barycentric drawing that later became the first mesh parameterization.

Mesh processing as a field grew in the 1990s, when scanners and simplification produced large triangle meshes. Pinkall and Polthier derived the cotangent formula in 1993. Taubin in 1995 viewed smoothing as filtering on the mesh graph and removed its shrinking with a second, negative step, and Desbrun, Meyer, Schröder and Barr in 1999 made the steps implicit with cotangent weights. Möller published his triangle-triangle test in 1997, and Cignoni, Rocchini and Scopigno released Metro in 1998 to compare simplified meshes.

Geodesics and parameterization followed two lines. Mitchell, Mount and Papadimitriou gave exact geodesics on polyhedral surfaces in 1987 and Chen and Han improved the bounds in 1990; Surazhsky and coauthors showed in 2005 that the exact method is practical. Crane, Weischedel and Wardetzky replaced exact propagation by two linear solves in the heat method of 2013. Floater used Tutte's system for surfaces in 1997 and gave mean value coordinates in 2003. Sheffer and de Sturler introduced angle-based flattening in 2001, and Lévy, Petitjean, Ray and Maillot least squares conformal maps in 2002.

Repair became a subject of its own with 3D printing and scanning. Barequet and Sharir closed gaps in CAD models with the triangulation dynamic program in 1995, and Barequet, Dickerson and Eppstein showed in 1998 that asking for a patch that does not cross itself is NP-complete. Liepa added the dihedral angle weight, refinement and fairing in 2003, Ju gave volumetric repair by grid parity in 2004, and the corner table of Rossignac, Safonova and Szymczak in 2001 made compact connectivity easy to program. Jacobson, Kavan and Sorkine-Hornung defined generalized winding numbers in 2013, and Zhou, Grinspun, Zorin and Jacobson combined them with exact arrangements in 2016 to compute Booleans of imperfect meshes. On the remeshing side, Botsch and Kobbelt reduced isotropic remeshing to four local steps in 2004, and Bommes, Zimmer and Kobbelt posed quad meshing as a mixed-integer problem in 2009.

## Further reading

- Mario Botsch, Leif Kobbelt, Mark Pauly, Pierre Alliez and Bruno Lévy, *Polygon Mesh Processing* (A K Peters, 2010). The standard textbook for the whole chapter, from data structures and smoothing to parameterization, remeshing and repair.
- Marco Attene, Marcel Campen and Leif Kobbelt, "Polygon mesh repairing: an application perspective", *ACM Computing Surveys* 45(2), 2013. A survey that sorts defects and repair methods by the use of the result, with the local and volumetric approaches side by side.
- Michael S. Floater and Kai Hormann, "Surface parameterization: a tutorial and survey", in *Advances in Multiresolution for Geometric Modelling* (Springer, 2005). The theory behind Tutte maps, mean value coordinates and conformal maps, with the proofs of injectivity.
- Alla Sheffer, Emil Praun and Kenneth Rose, "Mesh parameterization methods and their applications", *Foundations and Trends in Computer Graphics and Vision* 2(2), 2006. A wider survey of parameterization, including cutting, distortion measures and angle-based methods.
- Ariel Shamir, "A survey on mesh segmentation techniques", *Computer Graphics Forum* 27(6), 2008. The classification of segmentation into surface patches and parts, with the costs and algorithms of each.
- David Bommes and coauthors, "Quad-mesh generation and processing: a survey", *Computer Graphics Forum* 32(6), 2013. Cross fields, global parameterization and quad extraction, the context of mixed-integer quadrangulation.
