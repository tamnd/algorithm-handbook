20. In the plane a trivial motion is $S(q) = \omega J q + c$, with $J$ the turn by a right angle, and a bar with end $a$ and direction $u$ gives the functional $S \mapsto \omega\,(a \times u) + c \cdot u$. The three functionals are dependent exactly when some $S \ne 0$ is killed by all of them. If $\omega \ne 0$, $S$ is a rotation about a point $z$ and it is killed exactly by the lines through $z$; if $\omega = 0$, $S$ is a translation and it is killed by the lines perpendicular to $c$. So the bad cases are a relative rotation about the common point and a relative translation across parallel bars.
