1. ◦ Use the [Grashof condition](/grashof-condition/) to classify these four-bars. (a) Input 3, coupler 8, output 6, ground 7. (b) The same chain, with the link of length 3 as the ground. (c) Links of lengths 4, 9, 5 and 7, in any order.

2. ◦ A [slider-crank](/slider-crank-mechanism/) has crank $a = 3$, connecting rod $b = 10$ and offset $e = 2$. Find the stroke in exact form and as a decimal. Does the crank turn fully?

3. ◦ A four-bar has input $a = 4$, coupler $b = 10$, output $c = 7$ and ground $d = 9$. Give the coefficients $K_1, K_2, K_3$ of the [Freudenstein equation](/freudenstein-equation/) as fractions, classify the linkage, and solve for the output angle $\psi$ when the input angle is $\varphi = 0$.

4. ◦ How many [instant centers](/instant-center-of-velocity/) does a planar mechanism with 8 links have? For a [Watt six-bar](/watt-and-stephenson-six-bar/), how many centers are there in all, how many sit at the joints, and how many must be found with the [Aronhold-Kennedy theorem](/aronhold-kennedy-theorem/)?

5. ◦ Place four precision points on the input range $[0, 10]$ by [Chebyshev spacing](/chebyshev-spacing/). Give them to four decimals, and give the largest value of $|(x - x_1)(x - x_2)(x - x_3)(x - x_4)|$ on the range.

6. ◦ In [Burmester theory](/burmester-theory/), five positions give 4, 2 or 0 real Burmester points. How many four-bars does each case give? For a single dyad, how large is the family of solutions for three, four and five positions?

7. ◦ A cam turns at 600 rpm and raises its follower $L = 30$ mm over $\beta = 120°$ with the cycloidal [motion law](/cam-follower-motion-laws/). Find the peak velocity in mm/s and the peak acceleration in m/s².

8. ◦ Two [involute](/involute-gear-tooth/) spur gears of module $m = 3$ mm and pressure angle $20°$ have 18 and 45 teeth. Find the pitch radii, the standard center distance, the speed ratio with its sign, the base radii and the base pitch.

9. ◦ A rack with addendum $m$ cuts a gear. Using [gear undercutting](/gear-undercutting/), find the smallest tooth count free of undercut at $20°$ and at $25°$. Does an 11-tooth gear undercut at $25°$? What profile shift $x$ does a 10-tooth gear need at $20°$?

10. ◦ A planetary train has sun 30 teeth, planets 20 and ring 70. Check that the numbers fit, and find the train value $e$ from sun to ring in the [Willis formula](/willis-formula/). With the ring held and the sun at 1000 rpm, find the arm speed and the absolute planet speed. Can 3 equally spaced planets be fitted? Can 4?

11. • A four-bar has input $a = 2$, output $c = 5$ and ground $d = 6$. For which coupler lengths $b > 0$ is it a crank-rocker, for which a Grashof double rocker, and for which a triple rocker? Which values of $b$ give a change-point linkage?

12. • The lengths 2, 5, 6 and 7 can be joined in three cyclic orders, and each chain can stand on any of its four links. Classify all 12 linkages and count the double cranks, crank-rockers and double rockers.

13. • For the four-bar of exercise 3, find the [transmission angle](/transmission-angle/) at input angles $0°$ and $180°$. Does it meet the $40°$ rule of practice?

14. • For the slider-crank of exercise 2, find the crank angles of the two dead positions, the crank angles of the forward and the return stroke, and the time ratio. Use the angle convention of the entry.

15. • A centered slider-crank has $a = 2$, $b = 7$ and crank angle $90°$, with the crank turning at $\omega$. Where is the instant center of the connecting rod? Find the angular velocity of the rod and the slider speed.

16. • The four-bar of exercise 3 has fixed pivots $O_2 = (0, 0)$ and $O_4 = (9, 0)$, and the input is at $\theta_2 = 90°$. Using [vector loop closure](/vector-loop-closure/), find $\theta_3$ and $\theta_4$ on the circuit with $\theta_4 < 180°$. Then find $\omega_4/\omega_2$ and $\omega_3/\omega_2$ from the instant centers. What is $\omega_4/\omega_2$ on the other circuit?

17. • A body moves so that point $A$ goes from $(0, 0)$ to $(2, 0)$ and point $B$ goes from $(3, 0)$ to $(2, 3)$. Find the displacement pole and the rotation angle, as used in [motion generation](/motion-generation/).

18. • A moving pivot must pass through $(0, 0)$, $(4, 2)$ and $(6, 6)$. Find the fixed pivot and the crank length, as in [standard-form dyad synthesis](/standard-form-dyad-synthesis/) with three positions.

19. • A [Peaucellier-Lipkin linkage](/peaucellier-lipkin-linkage/) has long links $L = 6$, rhombus side $s = 2$ and a guide link of length $\rho$ that holds the inner point on a circle through the fixed pivot. How far from the fixed pivot is the line traced when $\rho = 2$? When $\rho = 4$?

20. • A radial roller follower rises $L = 20$ mm with simple harmonic motion over $\beta = 60°$. The prime circle has radius $R_p = 40$ mm. Find the largest [pressure angle](/cam-pressure-angle/) during the rise and where it occurs. Does it meet the $30°$ rule?

21. • For the gears of exercise 8 with standard addendum $m$, find the length of action and the [contact ratio](/gear-contact-ratio/). Is there interference? Then move the centers 1.5 mm apart and find the new operating pressure angle and contact ratio.

22. • In the train of exercise 10, the ring turns at 100 rpm and the sun at $-300$ rpm. Find the arm speed and the absolute planet speed.

23. • Four unit squares lie in a row and touch along their sides, and each can move only by a translation along $\pm x$. Build the [liaison graph](/liaison-graph/). Count the connected sets of parts, the two-handed plans of the [AND/OR graph](/and-or-graph-of-assembly-plans/), how many of them are linear, and the feasible linear orders.

24. • Three rectangles are $A = [0,1] \times [0,1]$, $B = [1,2] \times [0,1]$ and $C = [0,2] \times [1,2]$. For each of the directions $+x$, $+y$, $-x$ and $-y$, build the directional [blocking graph](/non-directional-blocking-graph/), with an arc $i \to j$ when $j$ blocks $i$, and list the sets that can move off. Which of the three splits into two parts can be made?

25. ★ A crank-rocker has crank $a = 2$, ground $d = 6$ and coupler equal to output, $b = c$. Choose $b$ so that the transmission angle departs equally from $90°$ at its two extremes. Give $b$, the formula for $\cos\mu$ in terms of the input angle, the range of $\mu$, and the Grashof class.

26. ★ Design a four-bar for [function generation](/function-generation/) through the precision points $(\varphi, \psi) = (30°, 60°)$, $(60°, 80°)$ and $(90°, 110°)$. Find $K_1, K_2, K_3$ to four decimals, the link lengths for ground $d = 10$, and the Grashof class. Can the input move from $30°$ to $90°$ without leaving the circuit? Give the transmission angle at each point.

27. ★ A four-bar has crank $a = 2$ at $O_A = (0, 0)$, coupler $b = 5$, rocker $c = 4$ at $O_B = (6, 0)$, and tracing point $P = A + z(B - A)$ with $z = 1 + i$, in complex notation, where $A$ and $B$ are the moving pivots. Use the [Roberts-Chebyshev theorem](/roberts-chebyshev-theorem/) to find the third fixed pivot and the link lengths and Grashof classes of both cognates. One cognate has a crank at the new pivot. By what constant angle does it differ from the original crank?

28. ★ Two equal gears with full-depth teeth, addendum $m$, mesh at the standard center distance. Show that they avoid interference exactly when $\tfrac34 \sin^2\varphi \, z^2 - z - 1 \ge 0$, and find the smallest tooth count at $25°$, $20°$ and $14.5°$.

29. ★ A planar frame has base $[0,3] \times [0,1]$, posts $[0,1] \times [1,2]$ and $[2,3] \times [1,2]$, a block $[1,2] \times [1,2]$ between the posts, and a lid $[0,3] \times [2,3]$ that touches the posts and the block. Parts move by one translation to infinity along $\pm x$ or $\pm y$, and both sides of each split must be connected. Count the liaisons, the connected sets, the orders in which each new part touches one already placed, how many of those orders are feasible, the two-handed plans and the linear plans. Which orders fail, and why?

30. ★ A radial roller follower rises $L = 15$ mm over $\beta = 120°$ with the 3-4-5 polynomial law. Find the smallest prime circle radius that keeps the pressure angle at or below $30°$, as in [cam profile synthesis](/cam-profile-synthesis/). Compare it with the cycloidal law, and with the same law over $\beta = 90°$.
