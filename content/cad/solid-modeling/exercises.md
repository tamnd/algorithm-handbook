1. ◦ In the plane, let $A = [0, 2] \times [0, 1]$, $B = [1, 3] \times [1, 2]$, and let $D$ be $A$ together with the segment from $(2, 1/2)$ to $(3, 1/2)$. Compute $A \cap B$, $A \cap^* B$, $A \cup B$, $A - B$, $A -^* B$ and the regularization $\operatorname{cl}(\operatorname{int} D)$ with the [regularized set operations](/regularized-set-operations/). Which of these sets are [r-sets](/r-set/)? Is a point of the segment $(1, 2) \times \{1\}$ an interior point of $A \cup B$?

2. ◦ In [function representation](/function-representation/), a solid is $\{x : f(x) \ge 0\}$. On the line, take $f_1(x) = -x$ and $f_2(x) = x$. Which sets do $\min(f_1, f_2) \ge 0$ and $\max(f_1, f_2) \ge 0$ describe? Which of them is regular, and what does the regularization $\operatorname{cl}\{f > 0\}$ give for each?

3. ◦ A [voxel model](/spatial-occupancy-enumeration/) is the $3 \times 3 \times 3$ block of unit cubes with the center cube removed. Count the cubes $n$ and the pairs $p$ of cubes that share a face, and compute the surface area as $6n - 2p$. How many shells does the boundary have?

4. ◦ The [sweep representation](/sweep-representation/) stores a solid as a planar profile and a motion. Extrude the triangle with vertices $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$, $(0, 2)$ in the plane $z = 0$ along $d = (1, 1, 2)$. Classify the points $(1, 1, 1)$, $(2, 1, 1)$, $(1, 1, 3)$ and $(1/2, 1/2, 0)$ as in, on or out, and give the volume. Then revolve the rectangle $1 \le r \le 3$, $0 \le z \le 2$ about the $z$ axis. Compute the volume as $2\pi m$ with $m = \frac16 \lvert \sum_i (r_i + r_{i+1})(r_i z_{i+1} - r_{i+1} z_i) \rvert$, check it against the volume of the ring, and classify $(0, 2, 1)$, $(3, 0, 1)$ and $(0, 0, 1)$.

5. ◦ A block has two square through holes side by side, each with four planar walls. Count $V$, $E$, $F$, the loops $L$, the rings $R = L - F$, the shells $S$ and the holes $H$, and check the [Euler-Poincaré formula](/euler-poincare-formula/) $V - E + F - R = 2(S - H)$.

6. ◦ Build a triangular prism with [Euler operators](/euler-operators/). How many MVFS, MEV and MEF operations do you need? For any solid of genus 0 with one shell and no rings, show that the counts are $1$, $V - 1$ and $F - 1$.

7. ◦ Let $A = [0, 4] \times [0, 2]$, $B$ the disk of radius 1 about $(2, 1)$ and $C = [7/2, 5] \times [1/2, 3/2]$, and let $S = (A -^* B) \cup^* C$. With [CSG ray casting](/csg-ray-casting/), give the intervals of the lines $y = 1$ and $y = 5/4$ that lie in $S$, leaf by leaf and node by node.

8. ◦ Grow the box $[0, 1] \times [0, 2] \times [0, 3]$ by a ball of radius $r = 1$ with [solid offsetting](/solid-offsetting/). Give the exact volume of the result. Then shrink the box by $r = 1/4$, and say for which $r$ the shrunk box is empty.

9. ◦ A triangular prism arrives from another system as five faces, each with its own copies of its corners and edges. How many vertex and edge records are there before [B-rep healing](/brep-healing/) sews them, and what are $V$, $E$ and $F$ after? Next, five copies of one corner lie on a line at $0$, $0.003$, $0.006$, $0.009$ and $0.012$, each with tolerance $t_0 = 0.001$. In [tolerant modeling](/tolerant-modeling/), two vertices merge when their centers are at most $t_u + t_v + s$ apart, here with $s = 0.002$, into the smallest ball that holds both. Merge the copies from left to right, and give the center and radius after each merge. How far apart are the end copies? For which spacings does a chain like this merge completely, whatever its length?

10. ◦ Take the set $D$ of exercise 1, the rectangle $[0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ together with the closed segment from $(2, 1/2)$ to $(3, 1/2)$. Build its [selective geometric complex](/selective-geometric-complex/), and count the vertices, edges and faces of the complex, the unbounded face included, and the marked ones. Do the same for $\operatorname{int} D$, $\partial D$ and $\operatorname{cl} \operatorname{int} D$, each after simplification. How many lines does the [Nef polygon](/nef-polyhedron/) of $D$ use, how many vertices, edges and cells does their arrangement have, and how many of each lie in $D$?

11. ◦ Build the [attributed adjacency graph](/attributed-adjacency-graph/) of the cube $[0, 4]^3$ with the blind pocket $[1, 3] \times [1, 3] \times [2, 4]$ cut from its top. Count the faces, the convex arcs and the concave arcs, list the faces that the method of Joshi and Chang deletes, and name the template that the remaining component matches. Do the same for the square through hole $[1, 3] \times [1, 3] \times [0, 4]$.

12. • The planes $x, y, z \in \{0, 1, 2\}$ bound the notched cube $[0, 2]^3 -^* [1, 2]^3$. Into how many open cells do the nine planes cut space, and how many of them lie in the solid? Write the solid as a union of cells, each an intersection of six half-spaces, and count the leaves. Then find a CSG formula in which each of the nine half-spaces appears once, as in [B-rep to CSG conversion](/brep-to-csg-conversion/).

13. • Let $A = [0, 2] \times [0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ and $B = [0, 1] \times [0, 1] \times [0, 2]$. After [boundary evaluation](/boundary-evaluation/) with coplanar faces merged, give $V$, $E$ and $F$ of $A \cup^* B$, check $V - E + F = 2$, and give its area and volume. Which faces are not convex?

14. • Let $A = [0, 2] \times [0, 1]$ and $B = [1, 3] \times [1, 2]$. With [set membership classification](/set-membership-classification/), split the segment from $(-1, 1)$ to $(4, 1)$ into its parts in, on and out of $A \cup^* B$. Give the classification with respect to each of $A$ and $B$ first, and say where the labels alone are not enough and neighborhoods are needed. Is the point $(1, 1)$ in or on?

15. • Viviani's curve is the intersection of $x^2 + y^2 + z^2 = 4$ and $(x - 1)^2 + y^2 = 1$. Start at $x_0 = (1.1, 0.9, 1.4)$ and make one corrector step $x \leftarrow x - J^{\mathsf T}(JJ^{\mathsf T})^{-1}F(x)$ of the least-length Newton method, with exact fractions. Then iterate in floating point until both residuals are below $10^{-12}$, and say how the residuals fall. Why does the method fail at the double point $(2, 0, 0)$?

16. • Take the torus with axis radius $R = 3$ and tube radius $r = 1$, with the outward normal. Give its principal curvatures in the sign convention of [offset surfaces](/offset-surface/), and find all $d$ for which the area factor $(1 - d\kappa_1)(1 - d\kappa_2)$ is positive at every point. Compare the answer with the bound $|d| < \rho_{\min}$.

17. • Round the equilateral triangle with inradius 1 by $r = 1/2$, that is, shrink it by $r$ and grow the result by $r$. How much area does it lose? Show that rounding any convex polygon with interior angles $\theta_i$ by a small enough $r$ loses $r^2(\sum_i \cot(\theta_i / 2) - \pi)$, and that filleting a convex polygon changes nothing.

18. • Two planes meet along a line, and the air between them is a wedge of angle $60°$. A [rolling ball](/rolling-ball-blend/) of radius 1 fills the concave edge. How far from the edge are the spine and the two contact lines? Through what angle does the blend arc turn, and how much material does the fillet add per unit length of edge?

19. • Find the [medial surface](/medial-surface-of-a-solid/) of the box $[0, 6] \times [0, 4] \times [0, 2]$: its junctions, its seams and its sheets, with the shape of each sheet. What is the radius of the largest ball inside the box, and where are its centers? Check that the junctions and box corners, minus the seams and box edges, plus the sheets, give 1, and say why. For which heights $h$ does the box $[0, 6] \times [0, 4] \times [0, h]$ give the same counts, and what happens at the others?

20. • In double precision, the points $p = (0.1, 0.4)$, $q = (0.3, 1.0)$, $r = (0.6, 1.9)$ lie on $y = 3x + 0.1$ as decimals, but not as stored. Evaluate $D(p, q, r) = (q_x - p_x)(r_y - p_y) - (q_y - p_y)(r_x - p_x)$ and its cyclic shifts $D(q, r, p)$ and $D(r, p, q)$ in floating point, and then exactly on the stored values with fractions. What does each say, and which answers are wrong?

21. • Compute the volume, the center of mass and the inertia tensor about the center of mass of the wedge $\{x, y \ge 0, x + y \le 2, 0 \le z \le 1\}$ of unit density, as in [mass properties of solids](/mass-properties-of-solids/). Use exact fractions.

22. • The section $[0, 10] \times [0, 6]$ has the two through holes $[2, 4] \times [2, 4]$ and $[6, 8] \times [2, 4]$. In [direct modeling](/direct-modeling/), its right face is pushed to $x_R = 10 - 9t$ for $t \in [0, 1]$, and each topology change is resolved by a Boolean with the swept region. Find the topology change points, the area of the section on each interval between them, and $V$, $E$, $F$, $R$, $H$ of the solid that the section extrudes on each interval. Check the Euler-Poincaré formula on each interval, and check that the area does not jump.

23. • In the planar model of [generic naming](/generic-naming-scheme/), the base is $[0, 12] \times [0, 6]$ and cut 1 is a pattern of two slots, $[2, 4] \times [3, 8]$ and $[8, 10] \times [3, 8]$. List the 12 edges of the boundary cycle with their labels, and find the level of each edge. An edit moves the second copy to $[3, 5] \times [3, 8]$, so that the two slots merge into one. Which of the old names resolve in the new model, and to which edges?

24. • Cut the block $W = [0, 8] \times [0, 6]$ by $D = [1, 5] \times [4, 6] \cup [3, 5] \times [1, 4]$, a wide slot with a deeper narrow slot at its right end, and let $P = W -^* D$. List the hints of [hint-based recognition](/hint-based-feature-recognition/), say which it drops, and give the features it returns. Find the maximal rectangles of $D$ on the grid and a minimum cover, as in [volume decomposition](/volume-decomposition-feature-recognition/). Run the [alternating sum of volumes](/alternating-sum-of-volumes/) on $P$ and check the areas. Finally, write $D$ in two ways as two rectangles with disjoint interiors, each open to the top, and explain with [feature interaction](/feature-interaction/) why both readings give the same part.

25. ★ Prove that the regular closed subsets of $\mathbb{R}^3$ satisfy $A \cap^* (B \cup^* C) = (A \cap^* B) \cup^* (A \cap^* C)$. Which step uses that $B$ and $C$ are regular?

26. ★ In function representation, the union with $\alpha = 0$ of the half-planes $x \ge 0$ and $y \ge 0$ is $f = x + y + \sqrt{x^2 + y^2}$. Show that the level set $f = 1$ is a branch of the hyperbola $(x - 1)(y - 1) = 1/2$. Where does it cross the diagonal $x = y$, and where does the curve of points at distance 1 from the complement of the union cross it? Answer the same for $\max(x, y) = 1$.

27. ★ Two unit cubes share one edge and nothing else. Count $V$, $E$ and $F$ of the union, show that no values of $S$ and $H$ satisfy the Euler-Poincaré formula, and explain the count with $\chi(A \cup B) = \chi(A) + \chi(B) - \chi(A \cap B)$ applied to the two boundary surfaces. Which data structure can store this solid, and why can the winged edge not?

28. ★ Generalize the volume of the grown box to a convex polyhedron $P$: show that $P$ grown by $r$ has volume $V + A r + \frac{r^2}{2}\sum_e \ell_e (\pi - \beta_e) + \frac43 \pi r^3$, where $\ell_e$ is the length and $\beta_e$ the dihedral angle of edge $e$. Compute it for the regular tetrahedron of edge 1 and $r = 1$, and check it by sampling points.

29. ★ The unit cube is the Minkowski sum of three unit segments along the axes. Add a fourth segment, from the origin to $(1, 1, 1)$, and find the facets of the sum from the candidate normals of [Minkowski sums of polyhedra](/minkowski-sum-of-polyhedra/): the facet normals of the operands and $\pm e \times f$ for edge directions $e$ and $f$. Count $V$, $E$ and $F$. Then prove that the sum of $k$ segments, no three of them parallel to one plane, has $k(k - 1)$ facets, all parallelograms, $2k(k - 1)$ edges and $k^2 - k + 2$ vertices. What is the sum when the fourth segment goes to $(1, 1, 0)$ instead?

30. ★ Generalize exercise 23 to a pattern of $k \ge 2$ slots cut into the top of a base rectangle, with the copies apart from each other and from the sides, so that the boundary is one cycle of $4k + 4$ edges. Show that the largest level is $2k - 1$, and find the edges that have it.
