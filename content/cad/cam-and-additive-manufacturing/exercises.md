1. ◦ A printer uses layers of thickness $t = 0.1$ mm. A facet of the part has the normal $(3, 0, 4)$, and the build axis is $z$. Find the cusp height that the [staircase effect](/staircase-effect/) leaves on this facet, and the cusp height when each layer is cut at its middle height instead of at its bottom.

2. ◦ Slice the octahedron $|x| + |y| + |z| \le 1$, as 8 triangles, with the rule of [slicing for additive manufacturing](/slicing-for-additive-manufacturing/) that a vertex at the height of the plane counts as above it. Which triangles does the plane $z = 0$ cut, and what is the contour? What do the planes $z = -1$ and $z = \tfrac12$ give? Give the area inside each contour.

3. ◦ Run greedy [adaptive slicing](/adaptive-slicing/) with the allowed thicknesses $0.1, 0.2, 0.3$ and the cusp bound $c = 0.1$ on a part that spans $0 \le z \le 1.8$. Below $z = 1.2$ all its side facets are vertical. Above $z = 1.2$ every side facet has $|\cos \theta| = \tfrac12$, and the top face is horizontal at $z = 1.8$. List the layer boundaries, and compare the number of layers with uniform layers of $0.1$.

4. ◦ An [infill pattern](/infill-pattern/) uses extrusion width $w = 0.4$ mm and line spacing $s = 4$ mm. Give the infill density of parallel lines and of a square grid. What spacing gives a grid of density 25%? What is the density of a honeycomb with edge $a = 2$ mm, by the entry's approximation?

5. ◦ The design surface has the contact point $p = (0, 0, 0)$ and the unit normal $n = (0, \tfrac35, \tfrac45)$, and the tool axis is $a = (0, 0, 1)$. Give the [cutter location data](/cutter-location-data/): the centre and the tip of a ball cutter of radius 5, the tip of a flat cutter of radius 5, and the tip of a torus cutter with $R = 5$ and corner radius $r_c = 1$.

6. ◦ A ball cutter of radius $r = 3$ mm cuts a plane with passes $w = 2$ mm apart. Find the exact [scallop height](/scallop-height/) and the approximation $w^2/(8r)$. Which stepover gives a scallop of 0.01 mm, by the approximation and exactly?

7. ◦ Compute the [inverse tool offset](/inverse-tool-offset/) for a ball cutter of radius 2 on a grid of spacing 1. The part is a floor at height 0 with a single pin of height 1 at the grid point $(0, 0)$. Give the tip height $Z$ at the grid points at distance 0, 1, $\sqrt2$ and 2 from the pin. How many grid offsets does the footprint of the tool cover?

8. ◦ A ray of a [dexel representation](/dexel-representation/) holds the intervals $I = [0, 3] \cup [5, 9]$ of material. A tool covers $J = [2, 6] \cup [8, 10]$ on the same ray. Give $I \setminus J$, $I \cup J$ and $I \cap J$ as sorted interval lists.

9. ◦ In [direct slicing](/direct-slicing/), a solid is the ball of radius 10 about the origin minus the infinite cylinder of radius 2 whose axis is the line through the origin with direction $(0, 4, 3)$. Find the two conics of the section by the plane $z = 3$, with the centre and the semi-axes of each. Which of the points $(0, 8)$ and $(0, 7)$ of the plane lie in the layer? With tol $= 0.01$, how many vertices does line L13 give each polygon? Why must the count for the hole use the semi-axis $10/3$ and not 2?

10. ◦ A [pencil cut](/pencil-cut-machining/) runs a ball of radius $r = 4$ along the valley where the floor $z = 0$, with unit normal $n_1 = (0, 0, 1)$, meets a sloped wall through the line $x = 10$, $z = 0$, with unit normal $n_2 = (-3, 0, 4)/5$. Both normals point into the free side. Find the opening angle $\alpha$ of the valley, the line of ball centres, its distance from the edge, and the two contact lines. How far from the edge is the centre when the wall is vertical, with $n_2 = (-1, 0, 0)$?

11. ◦ In [feedrate scheduling](/feedrate-scheduling/), a straight move of 60 mm starts and ends at rest, with the programmed feed $V = 40$ mm/s and the acceleration limit $A = 400$ mm/s². Give the least time and the distance spent accelerating. What changes for a move of 2 mm? Later the path has an arc of radius 2 mm, the interpolator period is 1 ms, the chord tolerance is 1 μm and the centripetal limit is $A_n = 400$ mm/s². Which of the three speed limits on the arc binds, and how far before the arc must the tool start braking from 40 mm/s?

12. • Use the facet test of [support structure generation](/support-structure-generation/) with the overhang angle $\alpha = 45°$: a facet needs support when $n_z < 0$ and $n_z^2 > \cos^2\!\alpha\, \|n\|^2$. Which of the facet normals $(0, 3, -4)$ and $(0, 4, -3)$ need support? Then use the layer view with $t = 0.25$ mm: the layer below is the square $[0, 20]^2$ and the layer above is $[0, 20] \times [0, 20.6]$. Find the region that needs support and its area.

13. • A part is the floor $y \le 0$ with a wall $x \ge 3$ that rises to $y = 4$ (work in the plane of the profile). A ball of radius 5 touches the floor at $p = (0, 0)$. Show that the local test of [gouge detection](/gouge-detection/) passes, but the ball gouges the wall. How far must the ball move straight up to clear the wall? What is the largest radius of a ball that touches the floor at $p$ without a gouge?

14. • A rectangular [pocket](/pocket-machining/) of $8 \times 6$ is cleared with a tool of radius $r = 1$. Give the region of tool centres, the uncut area, the area of the opening, and the lower bound on the path length of the toolpath planning entry. Then give the lengths of the [contour-parallel](/contour-parallel-toolpath/) offset loops of the boundary at distances 1 and 2, the distance at which the offset collapses, and what it collapses to. How many arcs does the medial axis of the rectangle have?

15. • An [iso-scallop toolpath](/iso-scallop-toolpath/) uses a ball of radius $r = 4$ mm and the scallop bound $h = 0.01$ mm. To first order, find the pass spacing on a plane, across a convex cylinder of radius 12 mm and across a concave cylinder of radius 12 mm. Then an [iso-planar toolpath](/iso-planar-toolpath/) on the plane $z = \tfrac43 x$ must keep the surface spacing found for the plane. What spacing of the drive planes, measured along $x$, does it need?

16. • A [z-map](/z-map-model/) has grid spacing 1 and all heights 5. A ball cutter of radius 2 on a shank moves its centre in a straight line from $(0, 0, 4)$ to $(6, 0, 1)$. Find the new heights at the grid points $(0, 0)$, $(2, 0)$, $(6, 0)$, $(7, 0)$ and $(2, 1)$. Explain why the height at $(2, 0)$ is lower than the bottom of the ball when the ball is right above that point.

17. • Three faces of a part have the outward normals $(1, 0, 0)$, $(0, 1, 0)$ and $(-1, -1, 1)$. Find a direction $d$ with $d \cdot n > 0$ for all three, so that one [accessibility cone](/accessibility-cone/) contains them all. Show that no such direction exists when a fourth face with normal $(0, 0, -1)$ is added.

18. • A cube $[0, 2]^3$ has a blind square hole $0.5 < y, z < 1.5$ drilled from the face $x = 0$ to the depth $x = 1$. Using the model of [mold parting directions](/mold-parting-direction/) with two halves that move in opposite directions and any parting surface, find every direction $d$ that works.

19. • Two parts in a stack have equal standard deviation $\sigma$ and correlation $\rho$. Find the variance of $G = X_1 - X_2$ and of $G = X_1 + X_2$. At $\rho = 1$, compare both with the value that the root sum of squares assumes.

20. • Run [bottom-left fill](/bottom-left-fill/), with holes allowed, on a strip of width 6. The rectangles (width, height) come in the order $(3, 2), (4, 1), (2, 3), (6, 1), (1, 2)$. Give the position of each, the height used, and decide whether the height is optimal. What height results if the rule never places a piece in a hole?

21. • A model for [multi-axis printing decomposition](/multi-axis-printing-decomposition/) is a gallows of 9 voxels: the column $(0, 0, z)$ for $0 \le z \le 4$ and the arm $(x, 0, 4)$ for $1 \le x \le 4$. Find $U(V, +z)$. List the support-free cuts in each of the five directions, with the size of each part and the number of base overhangs $|U(R \setminus P, +z)|$ that it leaves. With beam width $w = 1$, which child does line L9 keep, and what plan does the search return? Why is one part not enough?

22. • A tool of radius $R = 5$ clears a slot by [trochoidal milling](/trochoidal-milling/) with loop radius $\rho = 5$ and pitch $p = 1$. Give the slot width, the front engagement angle from the entry's formula, and the depth of a straight cut with the same engagement. Is the front angle the largest engagement along the loop? Then a [constant engagement toolpath](/constant-engagement-toolpath/) with the same tool must keep $60°$ while it cuts around a circle of radius $\rho = 20$. Find the stepover, and the engagement that the straight-wall stepover 2.5 would give there.

23. • A toroidal end mill with $R = 6$ and corner radius $r = 2$ finishes a concave cylindrical trough of radius $\rho = 40$ by the [principal axis method](/principal-axis-method/), feeding along the axis. Find the tilt $\lambda$ and check that $R_{\text{cut}}(\lambda) = \rho$. Does the no-gouge theorem apply? What tilt would a flat end mill of radius 6 need? Compare a ball end mill of radius 8 on the same trough: to second order, find the radius $\rho_{\text{eff}}$ with gap $x^2/(2\rho_{\text{eff}})$ across the feed, and the width of the strip where the gap is at most 0.01. The rule of [five-axis tool orientation](/five-axis-tool-orientation/), that a flat end at lead $\lambda$ cuts like a ball of radius $R/\sin\lambda$, helps here.

24. • Use [density filtering](/density-filtering/) on a grid of unit squares with $r_{\min} = 6/5$. Give the weights $H_{ef}$, and $D_e$ inside the grid, on an edge and in a corner. 25. ★ Find the least build height of a regular tetrahedron of edge 1 over all [build orientations](/build-orientation/), and a direction that achieves it. Compare it with the height when the tetrahedron stands on a face.

26. ★ The square $[-1, 1]^2$ rotates about its centre from $0°$ to $45°$. Find the area of its [swept volume](/swept-volume/).

27. ★ Three parts are in a chain with $|a_i| = 1$, and part $i$ costs $c_i / T_i$ when its tolerance is $T_i$. Under the worst case constraint $\sum T_i \le T$, derive the cheapest $T_i$, and compute them and the cost for $c = (1, 4, 9)$ and $T = 0.6$. Then solve the same problem under the RSS constraint $\sqrt{\sum T_i^2} \le T$, and compute the $T_i$ and the cost for $c = (1, 8, 27)$ and $T = 0.6$.

28. ★ Let $A$ be the U-shaped polygon with corners $(0, 0), (3, 0), (3, 2), (2, 2), (2, 1), (1, 1), (1, 2), (0, 2)$, and $B = [0, 1]^2$ with its reference point at the origin. Show that the translation $t = (1, 1)$ is a feasible placement whose interior does not meet the interior of $A$, yet $t$ lies in the interior of the [no-fit polygon](/no-fit-polygon/) $A \oplus (-B)$. Find all feasible $t = (1 + u, 1 + v)$ with $|u|, |v| < \tfrac12$.

29. ★ Two springs in series carry a load $f$. Spring $i$ has the stiffness $k_i x_i^p$, where $x_i > 0$ is its amount of material, $k = (1, 4)$, and $x_1 + x_2 \le 3$. The compliance is $c = f^2 (1/(k_1 x_1^p) + 1/(k_2 x_2^p))$. For $p = 1$, find the optimal $x$ and check the optimality condition of the [SIMP method](/simp-method/), equal sensitivities $-\partial c / \partial x_i$. Then find the optimum for general $p$ and for $p = 3$, and say whether this problem is convex.

30. ★ The [BESO method](/beso-method/) is tested on parallel bars between a wall and a rigid plate, with a unit load, $E = 1$ and compliance $1/\sum_e A_e/L_e$ over the solid bars. (a) The bars have area 1 and distinct lengths. Prove that the longest prefix of the bars sorted by length that fits in $V^*$ is an optimal solid set. Check this by brute force for the lengths 1 to 6 and $V^* = 10$. (b) Bar 1 has $L = 1$, $A = 1$, and bar 2 has $L = \ell > 1$ and area $A > \ell$. Take $V^* = A\ell$ and a removal ratio so small that the first target lies between $A\ell$ and $1 + A\ell$. Show that BESO keeps bar 1 alone, and find the ratio of its compliance to the optimum. Can BESO have a constant factor guarantee?
