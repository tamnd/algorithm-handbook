11. In [Abacus](/abacus-legalization/), cell 1 starts a cluster at 3. Cell 2 overlaps it, and the merged cluster has its best left edge at the mean of $3$ and $3 - 2$, which is 2. Cell 3 then overlaps, and the cluster of all three has its best left edge at $(3 + 1 + 0)/3 = \tfrac43$. The real positions are $\tfrac43, \tfrac{10}3, \tfrac{16}3$, with cost $\tfrac{14}3$. Rounding gives $1, 3, 5$, which is legal and has cost 5, and a search over all integer placements finds no smaller cost. [Tetris](/tetris-legalization/) gives $3, 5, 7$, with squared cost 13 and total displacement 5, against 3 for the rounded Abacus result.

12. The equations are $4a - b - 2c = 0$, $2b - a - c = 0$ and $4c - b - 2a = 12$, with solution $a, b, c = 5, 6, 7$. The quadratic cost is $25 + 1 + 1 + 25 + 2 \cdot 4 = 60$ and the weighted wire length is $5 + 1 + 1 + 5 + 2 \cdot 2 = 16$. The nets $Pa$, $ab$, $bc$, $cQ$ form a path from 0 to 12, so the linear length is at least 12. It is exactly 12 when $a = b = c$ anywhere in $[0, 12]$. The [quadratic model](/quadratic-placement/) spreads the cells because it penalizes the long nets $Pa$ and $cQ$ more than their linear length does.

14. The net $\{P, u, b\}$ has outside pins on both sides, so it crosses the cut whatever the split, and it is dropped and counted once. The pin $v$ lies on the cut line and gives no terminal, so $\{v, b, d\}$ becomes $bd$. The reduced netlist is $ab$, $bc$, $cd$, $ad$, $aT_0$, $cT_1$ and $bd$. Name a split by the pair on the left half. The plain cuts are 3 for $ab$, $ad$, $bc$ and $cd$ and 4 for $ac$ and $bd$. With terminals the cuts are 3 for $ab$ and $ad$ and 5 for the other four, and adding the dropped net gives the totals 4 and 6. So [terminal propagation](/terminal-propagation/) narrows four tied splits to the two that keep $a$ near $P$ and $c$ near $u$.

22. Suppose no segment runs from side to side. Then every segment has an end that lies inside another segment, which is perpendicular to it. Send each segment to such a segment. Following this map from any segment must repeat, so there is a cycle, and the orientations alternate along it, so its length is even. In a cycle of length 2, an end of $s$ lies inside $t$ and an end of $t$ lies inside $s$. Both are the crossing point of the two lines, but that point cannot be an end of $t$ and an inner point of $t$ at once. So a cycle has at least four segments. A [mosaic floorplan](/mosaic-floorplan/) with $n$ rooms has $n - 1$ segments, so it needs at least five rooms. Cut along the segment from side to side and apply the same argument to each part.

23. When every connected group of free cells has a net to a pad, the matrix is positive definite, so the minimum is unique. Swapping the coordinates of $u$ and $v$ in the minimizer gives a placement with the same cost, so it is the same placement, and $x_u = x_v$. The two cells overlap, and only a spreading step or a density term can separate them.

24. One tree has $A$ as root with left child $E$ and right child $D$. The other has $A$ as root with right child $D$, and $E$ as the left child of $D$: $E$ is placed just to the right of $D$, at $x = 1$, and drops to the floor. So several trees can encode one [B*-tree](/b-star-tree-floorplan/) packing, and a search over trees visits some packings more than once. Every admissible placement still has at least one tree.

25. Every split of a path cuts at least one edge, and $|A|\,|B| \le \lfloor n/2 \rfloor \lceil n/2 \rceil$, with both met by cutting the middle edge. For large $n$, $\lambda_2 = 2 - 2\cos(\pi/n) \approx \pi^2/n^2$, so $\lambda_2/n \approx \pi^2/n^3$, while the optimum is about $4/n^2$.

26. Suppose $x'_i \le x'_j$ but $i$ sits at site $q$ and $j$ at site $p$ with $p < q$. Swap them. With $a = x'_i$ and $b = x'_j$, the numbers $p - a$ and $q - b$ have the same sum as $q - a$ and $p - b$ and lie between them, so convexity gives $f(p - a) + f(q - b) \le f(q - a) + f(p - b)$. Each swap removes an inversion and does not raise the cost.
