11. $f(x) = 2.7 - 0.9 + 1.2 - 2 = 1$ and $\rho_\infty = 1/6$, but $x_2$ can rise only by $0.1$ before it reaches $1$. 24. Coordinate $i$ of $Wx + b$ is $\sum_j W_{ij} x_j + b_i$; each term ranges over $W_{ij}\mu_j \pm |W_{ij}| r_j$ independently, and the extremes are reached at a corner of the box. 25. Apply the definition to the event "member" and to its complement. The pair $(\mathrm{FPR}, \mathrm{TPR})$ then lies in a region bounded by two lines, and $\mathrm{TPR} - \mathrm{FPR}$ is largest where they cross, at $\mathrm{FPR} = 1/(e^\epsilon + 1)$. The bound is $\tanh(\epsilon/2)$: $0.245$, $0.462$ and $0.762$, against $0.649$, $1.718$ and $6.389$ for $e^\epsilon - 1$. Both are valid; the second is vacuous for $\epsilon \ge \ln 2$.

26. One inserted or deleted point lies in one part, so it changes one model and moves at most one vote, which lowers the gap by at most $2$. With gap $9$, $2r < 9$ gives $r = 4$.
