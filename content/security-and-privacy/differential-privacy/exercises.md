Unless an exercise says otherwise, neighbors differ by adding or removing one record (add/remove), $\mathrm{Lap}(b)$ has the density $(1/(2b))e^{-\lvert z\rvert/b}$, and $\ln$ is the natural logarithm. Give numbers to four significant figures where they are not exact.

1. ◦ Each record is a value in $[0, 10]$. Find the [global sensitivity](/global-sensitivity/) of each query under add/remove and under replace: (a) the number of records with value at least 5; (b) the pair (count of records, sum of values), in $\ell_1$ and in $\ell_2$; (c) the largest value, with the maximum of the empty dataset taken as 0; (d) the histogram of the values rounded to the 11 bins $0, 1, \ldots, 10$, together with the count of (a), in $\ell_1$ and in $\ell_2$.

2. ◦ The [Laplace mechanism](/laplace-mechanism/) answers a count with $\varepsilon = 0.5$. Give the noise scale, the probability that the noise exceeds 4 in absolute value, the half-width $t$ with $P(\lvert Z\rvert > t) = 0.05$, and the standard deviation. For a histogram of 20 disjoint bins with the same noise on each bin, give the $t$ such that all 20 bins are within $t$ with probability at least 0.9 by the union bound.

3. ◦ The [geometric mechanism](/geometric-mechanism/) adds $Z$ with $P(Z = d) = \frac{1 - \alpha}{1 + \alpha}\alpha^{\lvert d\rvert}$, $\alpha = e^{-\varepsilon}$, to a count. For $\varepsilon = \ln 3$, give $P(Z = 0)$, $P(Z = 1)$, $P(\lvert Z\rvert \ge 2)$ and the variance of $Z$, as fractions. Compare the variance with that of $\mathrm{Lap}(1/\ln 3)$.

4. ◦ In [randomized response](/randomized-response/) with $\varepsilon = \ln 4$, each person reports the true bit with probability $p = e^{\varepsilon}/(1 + e^{\varepsilon})$ and the other bit otherwise. Of $n = 1000$ people, 380 report 1. Give $p$, the unbiased estimate of the true fraction of 1s, the variance $p(1-p)/(n(2p-1)^2)$ of the estimate, and its standard deviation.

5. ◦ The [exponential mechanism](/exponential-mechanism/) picks one of three candidates whose vote counts are $(10, 8, 4)$; the utility is the count, with $\Delta = 1$, and $\varepsilon = \ln 2$. Give the three output probabilities as fractions with the weights $\exp(\varepsilon u/(2\Delta))$, and again with the weights $\exp(\varepsilon u/\Delta)$ that are allowed for a monotone utility.

6. ◦ An analyst spends a budget on one dataset under add/remove: a Laplace count at $\varepsilon = 0.3$; a 12-bin histogram on disjoint bins, Laplace at $\varepsilon = 0.4$ in each bin; a Gaussian answer that is $(0.2, 10^{-6})$-DP; and the same count as the first again, with fresh noise at $\varepsilon = 0.3$. Give the total by [sequential](/sequential-composition-theorem/) and [parallel](/parallel-composition-theorem/) composition. Then give the cost of the histogram alone if the neighbor relation is replace.

7. ◦ A mechanism is $(2, 10^{-6})$-DP. It is run on a Poisson sample that keeps each record with probability $q = 0.1$. By [amplification by subsampling](/privacy-amplification-by-subsampling/), give the new $\varepsilon$ and $\delta$. Compare the $\varepsilon$ with $q(e^{\varepsilon} - 1)$ and with $q\varepsilon$.

8. ◦ A mechanism is $(0.2, 10^{-7})$-DP under add/remove. By the [group privacy theorem](/group-privacy-theorem/), give its guarantee for groups of $k = 4$ records, with the exact slack $\delta(e^{k\varepsilon} - 1)/(e^{\varepsilon} - 1)$ and with the simpler bound $k e^{(k-1)\varepsilon}\delta$. Then give its guarantee under replace, where a replacement is a removal and an addition.

9. ◦ Ten thousand users hold values from a domain of $d = 100$ items, at $\varepsilon = \ln 5$ in the local model. Give the hash range $g = e^{\varepsilon} + 1$ of [optimized local hashing](/optimized-local-hashing/), its variance $4e^{\varepsilon}/(e^{\varepsilon} - 1)^2$ per user, and the standard deviation of one estimated count. Do the same for generalized randomized response, whose variance per user is $(d - 2 + e^{\varepsilon})/(e^{\varepsilon} - 1)^2$.

10. ◦ [RAPPOR](/rappor/) with $h$ hash functions and the permanent randomized response parameter $f$ protects one value with $\varepsilon_\infty = 2h\ln((1 - f/2)/(f/2))$. Give $\varepsilon_\infty$ for $f = 1/4$ with $h = 1$ and with $h = 2$. A user's value changes three times over the years, so that four distinct values are reported, each with $h = 2$. Give the bound on the total from sequential composition.

11. • A [binary tree mechanism](/binary-tree-mechanism/) releases the running count of a bit stream of length $T = 16$ at $\varepsilon = 1$, with $L = \lfloor\log_2 T\rfloor + 1$ and $\mathrm{Lap}(L/\varepsilon)$ on each node. Give $L$, the number of noisy nodes that make up the output at $t = 11$, $t = 15$ and $t = 16$, and the noise variance of each output. How many nodes does the whole run use? The simple method adds $\mathrm{Lap}(1/\varepsilon)$ to each bit and sums: give its variance at $t = 15$. For $T = 2^j$, the worst tree variance is $2j(j+1)^2/\varepsilon^2$ and the worst simple variance is $2(2^j - 1)/\varepsilon^2$; find the least $j$ for which the tree is better.

12. • Run the [sparse vector technique](/sparse-vector-technique/) with $\varepsilon = 0.5$, $\Delta = 1$, $T = 50$ and the query answers $f = (30, 44, 52, 61, 48, 70)$, using the comparison $f_i + \nu_i \ge \hat T$. (a) AboveThreshold, with the threshold noise $-3$ and the query noise $(9, -6, -7, 1, 4, -2)$: give the two noise scales and the output. (b) Sparse with $c = 2$, with the threshold noise $4$ and then $-6$ for the fresh threshold, and the query noise $(10, 3, -5, 2, -12, 6)$: give the two noise scales and the output. (c) Query 3 is above $T$ and is reported as $\bot$ in both runs. Does this contradict the accuracy bound $\alpha = 8\Delta(\ln k + \ln(2/\beta))/\varepsilon$ with $k = 6$ and $\beta = 0.1$?

13. • [Report noisy max](/report-noisy-max/) chooses between two candidates with counts $(6, 5)$ and $\mathrm{Lap}(1)$ noise on each, which is $1$-DP for counts under add/remove. Show that the first candidate wins with probability $1 - \frac{3}{4}e^{-1}$. On the neighbor where its count is 5, each wins with probability $\frac{1}{2}$. Give both log ratios of the output probabilities and check that they are at most 1.

14. • Each of $k$ mechanisms is $0.05$-DP. With $\delta' = 10^{-5}$, the [advanced composition theorem](/advanced-composition-theorem/) gives $\varepsilon' = \varepsilon\sqrt{2k\ln(1/\delta')} + k\varepsilon(e^{\varepsilon} - 1)$. Give $\varepsilon'$ for $k = 400$ and compare with basic composition. Find the least $k$ for which the advanced bound is smaller than $k\varepsilon$.

15. • A record can change each of 25 counts by at most 1. Calibrate the [Gaussian mechanism](/gaussian-mechanism/) at $\varepsilon = 0.5$, $\delta = 10^{-6}$ with the classical $\sigma = \Delta_2\sqrt{2\ln(1.25/\delta)}/\varepsilon$: give $\Delta_2$ and $\sigma$. Give the standard deviation of the Laplace mechanism at $\varepsilon = 0.5$ on the same counts. Explain why the classical formula may be used here.

16. • Find the error. A report says: "We release a count with Gaussian noise. The classical calibration with $\varepsilon = 10$ and $\delta = 10^{-6}$ gives $\sigma = 0.5299$, so the release is $(10, 10^{-6})$-DP. We round the noisy value to an integer, which keeps the guarantee by post-processing." Say which step is wrong and why. The exact profile of the Gaussian mechanism is $\delta(\varepsilon) = \Phi(\Delta/(2\sigma) - \varepsilon\sigma/\Delta) - e^{\varepsilon}\Phi(-\Delta/(2\sigma) - \varepsilon\sigma/\Delta)$: give $\delta(10)$ for $\sigma = 0.5299$, and the $\sigma$ that makes it $10^{-6}$.

17. • Two runs of randomized response with $p = 3/4$ on the same bit give the four outputs with the probabilities $(9, 3, 3, 1)/16$ on one input and $(1, 3, 3, 9)/16$ on the other. Compute the hockey-stick divergence $\delta(\varepsilon') = \sum_o \max(0, P(o) - e^{\varepsilon'}Q(o))$ at $\varepsilon' = 0$, $\ln 3$ and $\ln 9$. Then, by the [optimal composition theorem](/optimal-composition-theorem/) with $\delta = 0$, give the exact $\delta$ of three runs with $p = 2/3$ at $\varepsilon' = 0$, $\ln 2$, $\ln 4$ and $\ln 8$.

18. • One hundred queries of $\ell_2$ sensitivity 1 are answered with Gaussian noise of $\sigma = 10$. Target $\delta = 10^{-6}$. Give $\varepsilon$ from [zCDP](/zero-concentrated-differential-privacy/), with $\rho = k\Delta^2/(2\sigma^2)$ and $\varepsilon = \rho + 2\sqrt{\rho\ln(1/\delta)}$; from [Rényi DP](/renyi-differential-privacy/), with the curve $\alpha\rho$ and the conversion $\varepsilon = \alpha\rho + \ln(1/\delta)/(\alpha - 1)$ minimized over $\alpha$; and from [Gaussian DP](/gaussian-differential-privacy/), where the composition is $\mu$-GDP with $\mu = \sqrt{k}\,\Delta/\sigma$ and the exact $\varepsilon$ solves $\delta(\varepsilon) = 10^{-6}$ with the profile of exercise 16.

19. • At $\varepsilon = 1$ and $n = 10^4$, estimate the fraction of people with a bit equal to 1. Give the standard deviation of the randomized response estimate in the [local model](/local-differential-privacy/), and that of a Laplace count divided by $n$ computed by a trusted curator. Give their ratio and explain how it grows with $n$.

20. • Thirteen values in $[0, 100]$ are, sorted, $(10, 30, 44, 45, 46, 47, 48, 49, 50, 52, 54, 70, 90)$; the neighbor relation is replace. With the padding $x_i = 0$ for $i \le 0$ and $x_i = 100$ for $i > 13$, compute $A_k = \max_{0 \le t \le k+1}(x_{7+t} - x_{7+t-k-1})$ for $k = 0, \ldots, 4$. Give the distance to instability of [propose-test-release](/propose-test-release/) for $b = 5$ and $b = 10$, and the probability that the test passes at $\varepsilon = 1$, $\delta = 10^{-3}$ for $b = 10$. Then $A_6 = 52$ gives the largest term of the [smooth sensitivity](/smooth-sensitivity/) at $\varepsilon = 1$: give $S^*$, the Cauchy scale $6S^*/\varepsilon$, and compare with the median absolute error $100\ln 2$ of the Laplace mechanism with the global sensitivity 100.

21. • A curator splits the records by a rule that reads the data: if the dataset has an odd number of records, all records go to part 1, otherwise to part 2. It releases the two part counts, each with $\mathrm{Lap}(1/\varepsilon)$. Take $x = \{a\}$ and $x' = \{a, b\}$. Give the two pairs of true counts and the largest log ratio of the output densities. Explain why this does not contradict the parallel composition theorem.

22. • Prove that the geometric mechanism of exercise 3 is $\varepsilon$-DP for a query of sensitivity 1, and that $P(\lvert Z\rvert \ge t) = 2\alpha^t/(1 + \alpha)$ for integers $t \ge 1$.

23. • The bound of Feldman, McMillan and Talwar for [amplification by shuffling](/privacy-amplification-by-shuffling/) holds when $\varepsilon_0 \le \ln(n/(16\ln(2/\delta)))$, and then gives $\varepsilon \le \ln\big(1 + \frac{e^{\varepsilon_0} - 1}{e^{\varepsilon_0} + 1}\big(\frac{8\sqrt{e^{\varepsilon_0}\ln(4/\delta)}}{\sqrt n} + \frac{8e^{\varepsilon_0}}{n}\big)\big)$ under replace. With $\delta = 10^{-6}$, check the condition and give the bound for $\varepsilon_0 = 2$, $n = 10^6$. Does the theorem apply to $\varepsilon_0 = 4$, $n = 10^4$?

24. ★ Show that the Laplace mechanism with $b = 1$ on a query of sensitivity 1 is $(\varepsilon', 1 - e^{(\varepsilon' - 1)/2})$-DP for every $\varepsilon' \in [0, 1]$, and that this $\delta$ is the least possible. This is the privacy profile of [approximate differential privacy](/approximate-differential-privacy/).

25. ★ Let $k$ runs of randomized response with $p = e^{\varepsilon}/(1 + e^{\varepsilon})$ be applied to one bit. Show that the hockey-stick divergence between the two output laws is $\delta_k(\varepsilon') = (1 + e^{\varepsilon})^{-k}\sum_{a=0}^{k}\binom{k}{a}\max(0, e^{a\varepsilon} - e^{\varepsilon'}e^{(k-a)\varepsilon})$, the optimal composition formula with $\delta = 0$. Check it against exercise 17.

26. ★ With the [packing lower bound](/packing-lower-bound/), take $X = \{0, 1\}^4$, the 4 attribute means, $n = 20$ rows under replace and the error $\alpha = 0.05$. Build 16 datasets at distance at most $m = 3$ from one of them whose balls of radius $\alpha$ are disjoint, and show that no $\varepsilon$-DP mechanism with $\varepsilon < \ln 2$ answers the 4 means within 0.05 with probability $\frac{1}{2}$ on every dataset.

27. ★ The [matrix mechanism](/matrix-mechanism/) answers the workload $W = (1, 1)$, the total of two cells, through a strategy $A$ of full column rank, with total error $(2\Delta_A^2/\varepsilon^2)\operatorname{tr}(W(A^{\mathsf T}A)^{-1}W^{\mathsf T})$. Give the error of the identity strategy. For $A$ with the rows $(1, 1)$ and $(t, 0)$, $t > 0$, show that the error is $2(1 + t)^2/\varepsilon^2$, give it for $t = 1/4$, and find the $t$ for which $A$ beats the identity. What is the limit as $t \to 0$?

28. ★ A dataset holds one secret bit per person. Show that for every $\varepsilon$-DP mechanism under replace and every guesser that sees the output, the guesser gets a given person's bit right with probability at most $e^{\varepsilon}/(1 + e^{\varepsilon})$ when that bit is uniform and independent of the others, and that randomized response meets this bound. Relate the bound to the [Dinur-Nissim reconstruction attack](/dinur-nissim-reconstruction-attack/).
