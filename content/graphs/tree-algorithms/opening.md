Trees are the graphs on which almost everything is easy. A file system, an organization chart, a parse tree, the search tree of a game and a phylogeny are all trees, and so are the spanning trees, shortest-path trees and search trees of earlier chapters. With no cycles, every two vertices are joined by exactly one path, removing any edge splits the tree in two, and each subtree talks to the rest only through its root. Problems that are NP-hard on general graphs, such as the largest [independent set](/independent-set-on-trees/), are solved on trees by one pass from the leaves up.

The chapter first fixes the vocabulary and the [traversals](/tree-traversal/): [preorder](/preorder-traversal/), [inorder](/inorder-traversal/) and [postorder](/postorder-traversal/), and the [subtree sizes](/subtree-sizes/) that a postorder pass computes. Then come the measures of a tree's shape, the [diameter](/tree-diameter-double-sweep/), the [center](/tree-center-leaf-peeling/) and the [centroid](/centroid/), and [dynamic programming](/company-party-planning/) over subtrees. The middle of the chapter is about queries. The [lowest common ancestor](/lowest-common-ancestor/) of two vertices is the key to every question about the path between them. It is answered by [binary lifting](/binary-lifting/), by a range minimum over the [Euler tour](/euler-tour-lca/), or offline by [Tarjan's algorithm](/tarjan-offline-lca/). [Heavy-light](/heavy-light-decomposition/) and [centroid decomposition](/centroid-decomposition/) handle paths with updates. The chapter ends beyond trees themselves, with graphs that are close to trees ([treewidth](/treewidth/)), trees that summarize the paths of a flow graph ([dominators](/dominator-tree/)), and trees that approximate a metric ([FRT](/frt-tree-embedding/)).

## What the chapter covers

The chapter is in nine groups, read in order.

1. **Trees and rooted trees.** [Properties of free trees](/free-tree-properties/), the [edge count](/tree-edge-count/), [rooted trees](/rooted-tree/), [arborescences](/arborescence/), [binary](/binary-tree/) and [complete binary trees](/complete-binary-tree/), and the [Prüfer code](/pruefer-code/).
2. **Traversals.** [Tree traversal](/tree-traversal/), the [preorder](/preorder-traversal/), [inorder](/inorder-traversal/) and [postorder](/postorder-traversal/) traversals, [subtree sizes](/subtree-sizes/) and [Cartesian trees](/cartesian-tree/).
3. **Diameter, center and centroid.** [Eccentricity](/eccentricity/), the [diameter](/tree-diameter/) and the [double sweep](/tree-diameter-double-sweep/), the [center](/tree-center/) and [leaf peeling](/tree-center-leaf-peeling/), and the [centroid](/centroid/).
4. **Dynamic programming on trees.** The [company party](/company-party-planning/), [independent sets](/maximum-independent-set-in-trees/) [on trees](/independent-set-on-trees/) and the [rerooting technique](/rerooting-technique/).
5. **Ancestors and lowest common ancestors.** The [LCA](/lowest-common-ancestor/), [binary lifting](/binary-lifting/) and [LCA by lifting](/lca-binary-lifting/), the [Euler tour](/euler-tour-technique/) and [LCA by RMQ](/euler-tour-lca/), [Tarjan's offline LCA](/tarjan-offline-lca/), and [level ancestors](/level-ancestor-problem/) with [ladders](/ladder-decomposition/).
6. **Path and subtree queries.** [Path queries](/tree-path-queries/), [subtree queries](/euler-tour-subtree-queries/), [heavy-light decomposition](/heavy-light-decomposition/), [DSU on tree](/dsu-on-tree/), [centroid decomposition](/centroid-decomposition/) and [virtual trees](/virtual-tree/).
7. **Dominators.** The [dominators problem](/dominators-problem/), the [dominator tree](/dominator-tree/) and [Lengauer-Tarjan](/lengauer-tarjan-algorithm/).
8. **Tree decompositions and treewidth.** [Tree decompositions](/tree-decomposition/), [nice](/nice-tree-decomposition/) ones, [treewidth](/treewidth/), [path decompositions](/path-decomposition/), [computing treewidth](/treewidth-computation/), [constructing decompositions](/tree-decomposition-construction/) and [dynamic programming](/dp-on-tree-decompositions/) over them.
9. **Comparing, drawing and embedding trees.** [Tree edit distance](/tree-edit-distance/), [Zhang-Shasha](/zhang-shasha-algorithm/), [tree drawing](/tree-drawing/), [Reingold-Tilford](/reingold-tilford-layout/), [metric tree embeddings](/metric-tree-embedding/) and [FRT](/frt-tree-embedding/).
