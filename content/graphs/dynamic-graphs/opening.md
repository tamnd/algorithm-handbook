The graphs of earlier chapters were given once and then analysed. Real graphs change. Links in a network fail and come back, roads close, friendships form, a compiler adds edges to a dependency graph, and a database of facts grows. Recomputing connected components or shortest paths from scratch after every change costs at least linear time per update. A [dynamic graph](/dynamic-graph/) algorithm keeps enough structure between updates that each update and each query is much cheaper, often polylogarithmic.

The chapter begins with the setting: which updates are allowed ([insertions](/incremental-graph/), [deletions](/decremental-graph/) or [both](/fully-dynamic-graph/)), whether the sequence is known [in advance](/offline-dynamic-graph/), and how cost is measured, [amortized](/amortized-update-time/) or [worst case](/worst-case-update-time/). Insertions alone are easy for connectivity, which is [union-find](/incremental-connectivity/), and offline problems are solved by [undoing operations](/rollback-technique/) in stack order. The central tools are [dynamic trees](/dynamic-tree/), forests under link and cut: [link-cut trees](/link-cut-tree/) and [Euler tour trees](/euler-tour-tree/). On them stands fully dynamic [connectivity](/dynamic-connectivity/), in polylogarithmic time by the method of [Holm, de Lichtenberg and Thorup](/holm-de-lichtenberg-thorup/), with a matching [lower bound](/dynamic-connectivity-lower-bound/). The rest of the chapter covers spanning trees, directed reachability and topological order, shortest paths, and problems such as matching and coloring that are kept only approximately.

## What the chapter covers

The chapter is in nine groups, read in order.

1. **Graphs that change.** [Dynamic](/dynamic-graph/), [incremental](/incremental-graph/), [decremental](/decremental-graph/), [fully dynamic](/fully-dynamic-graph/) and [offline](/offline-dynamic-graph/) graphs, and [temporal graphs](/temporal-graph/).
2. **Measuring update cost.** [Amortized](/amortized-update-time/) and [worst-case update time](/worst-case-update-time/), and the [trade-off between updates and queries](/update-query-tradeoff/).
3. **Insertions only, and undoing them.** [Incremental connectivity](/incremental-connectivity/), [online bridge finding](/online-bridge-finding/), the [rollback technique](/rollback-technique/) and [offline dynamic connectivity](/offline-dynamic-connectivity/).
4. **Dynamic trees.** [Dynamic trees](/dynamic-tree/), [path queries](/dynamic-tree-path-queries/), [link-cut trees](/link-cut-tree/), [Euler tour trees](/euler-tour-tree/), [top trees](/top-tree/) and [rake-compress trees](/rake-compress-tree/).
5. **Connectivity under deletions.** [Decremental](/decremental-connectivity/) and [fully dynamic connectivity](/dynamic-connectivity/), [Holm-de Lichtenberg-Thorup](/holm-de-lichtenberg-thorup/), the [lower bound](/dynamic-connectivity-lower-bound/), [biconnectivity](/dynamic-biconnectivity/) and [bipartiteness](/dynamic-bipartiteness/).
6. **Spanning trees, sparsification and cuts.** The [dynamic minimum spanning tree](/dynamic-minimum-spanning-tree/), [topology trees](/frederickson-topology-trees/), [sparsification](/sparsification/), the [algorithm of Eppstein, Galil, Italiano and Nissenzweig](/eppstein-galil-italiano-nissenzweig/) and the [dynamic minimum cut](/dynamic-minimum-cut/).
7. **Directed graphs, reachability and order.** [Reachability](/dynamic-reachability/), [transitive closure](/dynamic-transitive-closure/) and [Italiano's algorithm](/italiano-dynamic-transitive-closure/), the [dynamic topological order](/dynamic-topological-order/) and [its incremental algorithm](/incremental-topological-ordering/).
8. **Shortest paths and spanners.** [Single-source](/dynamic-single-source-shortest-paths/) distances, [Ramalingam-Reps](/ramalingam-reps/), [decremental distances](/decremental-sssp/) and the [Even-Shiloach tree](/even-shiloach-tree/), [all pairs](/dynamic-all-pairs-shortest-paths/) and [Demetrescu-Italiano](/demetrescu-italiano-apsp/), and [spanners](/dynamic-spanner/).
9. **Matching, covers, coloring, density and planarity.** [Matching](/dynamic-matching/) and [Gupta-Peng](/gupta-peng-dynamic-matching/), [vertex cover](/dynamic-vertex-cover/), [coloring](/dynamic-graph-coloring/), [core numbers](/dynamic-k-core-decomposition/), the [densest subgraph](/dynamic-densest-subgraph/) and [planarity](/dynamic-planarity-testing/).
