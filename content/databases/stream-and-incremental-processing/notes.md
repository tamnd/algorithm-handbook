## History

Stored query results are as old as relational systems, but their maintenance became a research subject in the 1980s. José Blakeley, Per-Åke Larson and Frank Tompa gave delta rules for select-project-join views in 1986, with a test for updates that cannot change a view. In 1993 Ashish Gupta, Inderpal Singh Mumick and V. S. Subrahmanian published the counting algorithm for nonrecursive views and the DRed algorithm for recursive ones. Timothy Griffin and Leonid Libkin gave delta rules for the whole bag algebra in 1995.

Data warehouses made the subject practical. Views over remote sources had to be maintained without querying the sources, which led to self-maintainable views, studied in 1996 by Gupta, H. V. Jagadish and Mumick, and by Dallan Quass, Gupta, Mumick and Jennifer Widom. Latha Colby and her colleagues described deferred maintenance in the same year, including the error of computing a delta against the state after the update.

Later work made incremental computation a general method. Christoph Koch showed in 2010 that the delta of a delta is simpler again, and Yanif Ahmad, Oliver Kennedy, Koch and Milos Nikolic built higher-order maintenance into the DBToaster system in 2012. Christoph Berkholz, Jens Keppeler and Nicole Schweikardt characterized in 2017 the conjunctive queries whose results can be maintained with constant time per update. Frank McSherry, Derek Murray, Rebecca Isaacs and Michael Isard described differential dataflow in 2013, and, with Paul Barham and Martín Abadi, the timely dataflow system Naiad that runs it. Mihai Budiu, Tej Chajed, McSherry, Leonid Ryzhyk and Val Tannen gave DBSP in 2023, an algebra of streams in which every query of the relational algebra, and recursive ones, has an incremental form.

Queries over streams have their own line. Douglas Terry, David Goldberg, David Nichols and Brian Oki defined continuous queries over append-only databases for the Tapestry system in 1992. At Stanford, Brian Babcock, Shivnath Babu, Mayur Datar, Rajeev Motwani and Widom set out the problems of data stream systems in 2002, and Arvind Arasu, Babu and Widom gave the semantics of CQL in 2006, the language of their STREAM system. Babcock, Babu, Datar and Motwani gave Chain scheduling in 2003. Peter Tucker, David Maier, Tim Sheard and Leonidas Fegaras described punctuation in 2003, for the Niagara system. The symmetric hash join goes back to Annita Wilschut and Peter Apers in 1991, and Tolga Urhan and Michael Franklin made it spill to disk as XJoin in 2000.

Window aggregation was shared step by step: panes by Jin Li, Maier, Kristin Tufte, Vassilis Papadimos and Tucker in 2005; sharing across queries by Sailesh Krishnamurthy, Chung Wu and Franklin in 2006; FlatFAT by Kanat Tangwongsan, Martin Hirzel, Scott Schneider and Kun-Lung Wu in 2015; and slicing for user-defined windows in Cutty (2016) and Scotty (2021), by groups that included Paris Carbone, Jonas Traub and Volker Markl. Event patterns were matched with stacks of active instances in SASE by Eugene Wu, Yanlei Diao and Shariq Rizvi in 2006, and SASE+ named the selection strategies in 2008.

At Google, Tyler Akidau and his colleagues described low watermarks in MillWheel in 2013, and the Dataflow Model of windows, triggers and accumulation in 2015. Carbone, Gyula Fóra, Stephan Ewen, Seif Haridi and Kostas Tzoumas adapted the snapshot of K. Mani Chandy and Leslie Lamport (1985) to dataflow jobs in 2015, as asynchronous barrier snapshotting in Apache Flink.

## Further reading

- Ashish Gupta, Inderpal Singh Mumick and V. S. Subrahmanian, *Maintaining Views Incrementally*, SIGMOD 1993. Counting and DRed.
- Ashish Gupta and Inderpal Singh Mumick, *Maintenance of Materialized Views: Problems, Techniques, and Applications*, IEEE Data Engineering Bulletin 18(2), 1995. A short survey of the field.
- Christoph Koch, *Incremental Query Evaluation in a Ring of Databases*, PODS 2010. Higher-order delta processing.
- Mihai Budiu, Tej Chajed, Frank McSherry, Leonid Ryzhyk and Val Tannen, *DBSP: Automatic Incremental View Maintenance for Rich Query Languages*, PVLDB 16(7), 2023. Incremental computation as an algebra of streams.
- Arvind Arasu, Shivnath Babu and Jennifer Widom, *The CQL Continuous Query Language: Semantic Foundations and Query Execution*, VLDB Journal 15(2), 2006. Streams, relations and the operators between them.
- Tyler Akidau and others, *The Dataflow Model*, PVLDB 8(12), 2015. Event time, windows, watermarks and triggers.
