## History

The Phred scale comes from capillary sequencing. Brent Ewing and Phil Green described the phred base caller in two papers in Genome Research in 1998, the second of which calibrated its qualities as error probabilities, $Q = -10 \log_{10} e$. The scale outlived the machines it was made for. When short-read sequencing arrived, Heng Li, Jue Ruan and Richard Durbin built Maq (2008) around it: they defined the mapping quality of a read as a posterior probability on the same scale, and they used base and mapping qualities together to call SNPs. The SAM format and SAMtools (Li and others, 2009) and the Variant Call Format with VCFtools (Danecek and others, 2011), both from work on the 1000 Genomes Project, fixed the files that every tool now reads and writes.

The Genome Analysis Toolkit (McKenna and others, 2010) and the framework paper of DePristo and others (2011) turned calling into a pipeline: duplicate marking, local realignment, base quality recalibration, genotyping and the Gaussian mixture filter VQSR. Haplotype-based callers replaced the column-by-column view near indels. Erik Garrison and Gabor Marth described FreeBayes in 2012, and the GATK HaplotypeCaller, with local de Bruijn assembly and a pair HMM, was described by Poplin and others in 2017. Adrian Tan, Goncalo Abecasis and Hyun Min Kang (2015) defined the normalized form of a variant and proved that it is unique. Somatic callers such as Strelka (Saunders and others, 2012) and MuTect (Cibulskis and others, 2013) were built to compare a tumor with a matched normal.

Haplotype assembly was posed as a combinatorial problem in the early 2000s. Lippert, Schwartz, Lancia and Istrail (2002) surveyed the objectives, among them minimum error correction, and Cilibrasi, van Iersel, Kelk and Tromp (2007) settled the complexity of several of them, including the NP-hardness of MEC for gapless fragments. Bansal and Bafna gave the heuristic HapCUT in 2008. Patterson and others (2015) described WhatsHap, the dynamic program whose cost is exponential in the coverage only, made for long reads.

Structural variation from paired ends began with clone ends: Tuzun and others (2005) used fosmid pairs, and Korbel and others (2007) used paired-end mapping of next-generation reads. BreakDancer (Chen and others, 2009) clustered discordant pairs, Pindel (Ye and others, 2009) found breakpoints from split reads by pattern growth, and DELLY (Rausch and others, 2012) combined the two signals. Copy number methods came from array CGH. Olshen, Venkatraman, Lucito and Wigler introduced circular binary segmentation in 2004, and Venkatraman and Olshen made it faster in 2007. Fridlyand and others (2004) fitted HMMs to array data, and PennCNV (Wang and others, 2007) did so for SNP arrays. For sequencing, Yoon and others (2009) corrected read depth for GC content, and CNVnator (Abyzov and others, 2011) segmented the corrected depth. The variation graph toolkit vg (Garrison and others, 2018) mapped reads to a graph of known variants instead of one linear reference.

## Further reading

- Rasmus Nielsen, Joshua S. Paul, Anders Albrechtsen and Yun S. Song, "Genotype and SNP calling from next-generation sequencing data", Nature Reviews Genetics 12 (2011). A short review of genotype likelihoods, priors and the pitfalls of low coverage.
- Can Alkan, Bradley P. Coe and Evan E. Eichler, "Genome structural variation discovery and genotyping", Nature Reviews Genetics 12 (2011). The four signals of structural variant detection and what each can and cannot see.
- Mark A. DePristo and others, "A framework for variation discovery and genotyping using next-generation DNA sequencing data", Nature Genetics 43 (2011). The pipeline of recalibration, calling and filtering in one paper.
- Heng Li, Jue Ruan and Richard Durbin, "Mapping short DNA sequencing reads and calling variants using mapping quality scores", Genome Research 18 (2008). The source of mapping quality and a clear model of calling from qualities.
- The SAM/BAM Format Specification and the Variant Call Format Specification (hts-specs). The exact meaning of every field, FLAG bit and CIGAR operation.
