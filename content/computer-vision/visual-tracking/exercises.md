1. ◦ Score a tracker with the [CLEAR MOT metrics](/clear-mot-metrics/) at threshold $T = 0.5$. Two objects $o_1, o_2$ are present in frames 1 to 3. Frame 1: $h_1$ on $o_1$ with IoU 0.8, $h_2$ on $o_2$ with IoU 0.7. Frame 2: $h_1$ on $o_1$ with IoU 0.6, $h_2$ on $o_2$ with IoU 0.4, $h_3$ on $o_2$ with IoU 0.9. Frame 3: no hypothesis near $o_1$, $h_3$ on $o_2$ with IoU 0.75, and $h_4$ on nothing. All other pairs have IoU 0. Count the misses, false positives and switches, and give MOTA and MOTP. Repeat with $T = 0.35$.

2. ◦ For the [IDF1 score](/idf1-score/), the true trajectories have $|o_1| = 10$ and $|o_2| = 8$ frames, and the computed ones $|h_1| = 8$, $|h_2| = 6$ and $|h_3| = 5$. The agreements are $n(o_1, h_1) = 6$, $n(o_1, h_3) = 4$, $n(o_2, h_2) = 5$, $n(o_2, h_3) = 1$, and 0 for the other pairs. Find the best matching, IDTP, IDP, IDR and IDF1.

3. ◦ At one threshold $\alpha$ of [HOTA](/hota-metric/), one object is present in 4 frames. The tracker matches it with identity $p_1$ in frames 1 to 3 and with $p_2$ in frame 4, and gives one more box $p_3$, matched to nothing, in frame 2. Compute $\mathrm{DetA}_\alpha$, $\mathrm{AssA}_\alpha$ and $\mathrm{HOTA}_\alpha$.

4. ◦ A track predicts $\hat z = (0, 0)$ with $S = \begin{pmatrix} 4 & 2 \\ 2 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$, and the [gate](/track-gating/) is $\gamma = 9.21$. Two measurements lie at the offsets $(4, 4)$ and $(4, -2)$. Compute their squared Mahalanobis distances and their Euclidean distances, and say which passes. Which of them passes the coordinate box $|\nu_k| \le \sqrt{\gamma S_{kk}}$? Give the area of the gate.

5. ◦ A [Kalman filter](/kalman-filter/) tracks a point on a line with state $(p, v)$, $F = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 1 \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$, $H = (1, 0)$, $Q = \operatorname{diag}(0, 0.5)$ and $R = 2$. From $x = (0, 2)$ and $P = I$, predict, then update with $z = 3.2$. Give $S$, the gain, the new state, the new covariance and the squared Mahalanobis distance of $z$. Under which assumptions is the result the exact posterior?

6. ◦ In the stereo form of [scene flow](/scene-flow/), $f = 500$ pixels and $b = 0.2$ m. A pixel at $(u, v) = (100, 50)$, measured from the principal point, has disparity 20. Its flow is $(10, 0)$ and the disparity at the new position is 25. Compute $\mathbf X$, $\mathbf X'$ and $\Delta \mathbf X$. Explain why $u$ grows while $X$ shrinks.

7. ◦ A pixel of the [Stauffer-Grimson model](/stauffer-grimson-background-model/) has the components $(w, \mu, \sigma^2) = (0.6, 100, 16)$, $(0.3, 150, 25)$ and $(0.1, 60, 400)$, with $\alpha = 0.01$ and $T = 0.7$. The value 108 arrives. Which component matches, what are the new weights, and is the pixel background? Then the value 135 arrives at the original model. What happens, with $w_0 = 0.05$ and $v_0 = 900$?

8. ◦ A [ViBe](/vibe-background-subtraction/) pixel has the samples $(85, 97, 118, 130, 60, 104)$, read in this order, with $R = 20$ and $\#_{\min} = 2$. Classify the values 100, 125, 75 and 140, and count the samples read. With $N = 20$ and $\varphi = 16$, what is the probability that a sample of an isolated background pixel survives 100 frames?

9. ◦ The probability image of [CAMShift](/camshift/) is 255 at the six pixels $(x, y)$ with $x \in \{10, 11, 12\}$ and $y \in \{10, 11\}$, and 0 elsewhere in the window. Compute $M_{00}$, the centroid, the side $s$ of the next window, the angle and the two spreads $l$ and $w$.

10. ◦ In one dimension, [mean shift tracking](/mean-shift-tracking/) has a window of half width $h = 3$ centered at $y_0 = 3$, covering the pixels 1 to 5, with the Epanechnikov profile $k(r) = 1 - r^2$. Pixels 1 and 2 fall in bin A, pixels 3, 4 and 5 in bin B. The target histogram is $q = (0.75, 0.25)$. Compute the candidate histogram $p$, the Bhattacharyya coefficient, the weights and the new center $y_1$.

11. • [Global nearest neighbor](/global-nearest-neighbor-association/) association has two tracks A, B and two measurements, with squared distances $A z_1 = 1$, $A z_2 = 2$, $B z_1 = 1.5$ and $B z_2 = 7$, a gate $\gamma = 9.21$ and $c_{\text{miss}} = c_{\text{new}} = \gamma / 2$. Write the $4 \times 4$ augmented matrix and find the optimal assignment. Compare with the rule that takes the closest pair first. Repeat with $\gamma = 6$.

12. • In [JPDA](/joint-probabilistic-data-association/), tracks A and B both gate $z_1$ and $z_2$, with likelihoods $L_{A1} = 0.2$, $L_{A2} = 0.05$, $L_{B1} = L_{B2} = 0.1$, $p_D = 0.8$, $p_G = 1$ and $\lambda = 0.02$. List the joint events with their weights and compute all $\beta$. Compare $\beta_{B1}$ and $\beta_{B2}$ with the weights that B would get if it were alone.

13. • [ByteTrack](/bytetrack/) with $\tau = 0.6$ and $s_{\min} = 0.1$ has three tracked tracks predicting $A = (0,0,10,10)$, $B = (20,0,30,10)$ and $C = (40,0,50,10)$. The detections are $(1,0,11,10)$ with score 0.9, $(22,0,32,10)$ with 0.35, $(60,0,70,10)$ with 0.8 and $(41,0,51,10)$ with 0.05. Run both rounds, and say which tracks are updated, which become lost and whether a new track starts. What does a tracker that keeps only boxes above $\tau$ do?

14. • In the matching cascade of [Deep SORT](/deep-sort/), track 1 was last seen 3 frames ago and track 2 one frame ago. With $\lambda = 0$, the appearance costs are $1a = 0.05$, $1b = 0.25$, $2a = 0.10$ and $2b = 0.15$, all admissible. Give the result of the cascade and of one assignment over all tracks. Then compute an appearance cost from embeddings: track 1 keeps the unit embeddings $(1, 0)$ and $(0.8, 0.6)$ and a detection has the embedding $(0.6, 0.8)$; give its cosine distance to the track.

15. • [Multiple hypothesis tracking](/multiple-hypothesis-tracking/) uses Murty's method to list assignments in order of cost. For the matrix $\begin{pmatrix} 1 & 4 & 6 \\ 3 & 2 & 7 \\ 5 & 8 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$, run it until three assignments are reported. Give each subproblem with its constraints and its best solution.

16. • In [network flow tracking](/network-flow-tracking/), frame 0 has detections $a, b$, frame 1 has $c$, and frame 2 has $d, e$. Each detection costs $-2$, entry and exit cost 1 each. The links cost $a \to c$: 0.5, $b \to c$: 0.3, $c \to d$: 0.4, $c \to e$: 0.6, and the gap links $a \to d$: 1.2 and $b \to e$: 1.0. Run successive shortest paths and give the cost of each path, the tracks and the total. Give the least cost $F(k)$ for $k = 0, 1, 2, 3$ tracks.

17. • In one dimension, the [Gaussian mixture PHD filter](/phd-filter/) has two predicted components, weights 0.9 and 0.5 at the means 0 and 10, each with $S = 1$ after $H$. With $p_D = 0.9$ and clutter intensity $\kappa = 0.05$, one measurement $z = 0.5$ arrives. Compute the updated weights, the expected number of targets, and which components are reported. What is the count when no measurement arrives?

18. • The [MOSSE filter](/mosse-filter/) updates $A$ and $B$ with $\eta = 0.125$. What weight does the frame 10 steps back have? How many recent frames hold half of the total weight, and after how many frames does the starting model weigh less than 1%? With $\eta = 1/2$, one frequency, $\varepsilon = 0$, a first frame $\hat f = 1$, a second frame $\hat f = 3$ and $\hat g = 1$ in both, compare $A / B$ with the running mean of the per-frame filters.

19. • A window of the [KLT tracker](/kanade-lucas-tomasi-tracker/) has four pixels with gradients $(2, 0)$, $(2, 1)$, $(1, 1)$ and $(1, 0)$, and differences $I - J$ of 2, 3, 1 and 1. Compute $G$, its eigenvalues and the translation step $d = G^{-1} \sum (I - J) \nabla J$. Does the window pass the Shi-Tomasi test with threshold 2?

20. • Run [inverse compositional alignment](/inverse-compositional-alignment/) for a translation $W(x; p) = x + p$ on the points $x = 1, 2, 3$, with $T(x) = x^2$ and $I(x) = (x - 0.5)^2$, starting at $p = 0$. The steepest descent image is $T'(x) = 2x$. Give $H$ and the first two steps. Show that the error $\delta = p - 0.5$ obeys $\delta_{k+1} = -\frac{12}{56} \delta_k^2$.

21. • A tracker gives six point tracks with the displacements $d_x = (2, 2.1, 1.9, 2, 7, -3)$ and $d_y = (1, 0.9, 1.1, 1.2, 0, 4)$, and the [forward-backward errors](/forward-backward-error/) $(0.1, 0.3, 0.2, 0.15, 5, 3.5)$. Keep the points whose error is at most the median error, and give the median displacement of the kept points. Compare with the median of all six.

22. • One object is visible for $T$ frames with exact boxes. The tracker uses identity $a$ for the first $fT$ frames and $b$ for the rest, with $0 < f < 1$. Give MOTA, IDF1 and HOTA as functions of $f$ and $T$. Evaluate them for $f = 1/2$ and $f = 0.9$, and show that here HOTA is never below IDF1.

23. ★ Show that the number of joint events of JPDA with $n$ tracks and $m$ measurements, all in every gate, is $E(n, m) = \sum_{k} \binom{n}{k} \binom{m}{k} k!$, and that $E(n, m) = E(n - 1, m) + m\, E(n - 1, m - 1)$. Compute $E(5, 5)$ and $E(10, 10)$, and compare them with $n!$.

24. ★ In [tracking by detection](/tracking-by-detection/), suppose every detection must lie on a track, every track pays $c_{\text{en}} + c_{\text{ex}}$, and links join only consecutive frames. Show that the best set of tracks is found by solving each pair of consecutive frames on its own as an assignment with link costs $c_{ij} - c_{\text{en}} - c_{\text{ex}}$. Then add one gap link: frame 0 has $a_1, a_2$, frame 1 has $b_1$, frame 2 has $c_1, c_2$, the links cost $a_1 b_1 = 1$, $a_2 b_1 = 1.1$, $b_1 c_1 = 1$, $b_1 c_2 = 3$ and $a_1 c_2 = 0.2$, and $c_{\text{en}} + c_{\text{ex}} = 4$. Compare the frame by frame result with the optimum.

25. ★ The [CONDENSATION algorithm](/condensation-algorithm/) selects with $N$ independent draws. Systematic resampling draws one $u$ uniform in $[0, 1/N)$ and takes the samples at the points $u + (n - 1)/N$. Show that sample $k$ is then copied $\lfloor N \pi_k \rfloor$ or $\lceil N \pi_k \rceil$ times, with mean $N \pi_k$. For $\pi = (0.5, 0.3, 0.2)$ and $N = 4$, give the distribution of the copy counts and compare the variances with the multinomial ones.

26. ★ A track follows a random walk with process variance $q$ and measurement variance $r$. Show that the predicted variance of the Kalman filter, $P^-_{k+1} = P^-_k r / (P^-_k + r) + q$, converges from every $P^-_0 \ge 0$ to $(q + \sqrt{q^2 + 4 q r}) / 2$. For $q = 1$ and $r = 2$ give the limit, the steady gain, $S$, and the half width of a gate that keeps 99% of the true measurements ($\chi^2_1$ quantile 6.635).

27. ★ A pixel of the Stauffer-Grimson model flickers between two values $A$ and $B$ in alternate frames, far apart, and the two components keep equal variances that do not change. Show that, from any start, the weight of the component just matched tends to $1/(2 - \alpha)$ and the other to $(1 - \alpha)/(2 - \alpha)$. Deduce that the matched value is background for every $T < 1$ in the limit.
