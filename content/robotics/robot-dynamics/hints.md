27. By invariance under translations and rotations, the angular momentum about the center of mass is $k = a(\varphi)\dot\theta + b(\varphi)\dot\varphi$ with $a(\varphi) > 0$, the inertia of the locked pair. With $k = 0$, $\dot\theta = -\tfrac{b(\varphi)}{a(\varphi)}\dot\varphi$, so $\theta$ changes by $F(\varphi_{\mathrm{end}}) - F(\varphi_{\mathrm{start}})$ with $F' = -b/a$, which is 0 for a closed cycle. With two joint angles, the form $-(b_1\,d\varphi_1 + b_2\,d\varphi_2)/a$ need not be closed, and the change of $\theta$ around a loop is in general not zero.
