1. ◦ A rigid body has the principal inertia $\mathrm{diag}(2, 3, 4)$ kg m$^2$ in a body frame at its center of mass, and it turns with the body angular velocity $\omega_b = (0, 1, 1)$ rad/s. Use the [Newton-Euler equations](/newton-euler-equations/) to find the moment that keeps $\omega_b$ constant, and the angular acceleration $\dot\omega_b$ when no moment acts.

2. ◦ The planar arm with two revolute joints, links of 1 m and point masses of 1 kg at the link ends has the [joint-space inertia matrix](/joint-space-inertia-matrix/) $M = \begin{pmatrix} 3 + 2\cos q_2 & 1 + \cos q_2 \\ 1 + \cos q_2 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$. Give $M$ and $\det M$ at $q_2 = 2\pi/3$, and the kinetic energy for $\dot q = (1, -1)$ rad/s there.

3. ◦ A Cartesian gantry has three prismatic joints: the first carries all three moving parts, the second carries the second and third, and the third, vertical, carries only the tool holder. The moving masses are $5$, $3$ and $2$ kg, and gravity is $9.81$ m/s$^2$ along the third joint. Give the [manipulator equation](/manipulator-equation/) and the joint forces for $\ddot q = (1, 2, -1)$ m/s$^2$ at any velocity.

4. ◦ Which of these triples can be the principal moments of inertia of a rigid body about its center of mass, and what kind of body does each valid one need: $(2, 3, 4)$, $(1, 2, 3)$, $(1, 1, 3)$, $(0, 1, 1)$? Use the conditions in [spatial inertia](/spatial-inertia/).

5. ◦ A point mass of 2 kg sits at $c = (0, 1, 0)$ m in a frame. Write the three blocks of its spatial inertia about the frame origin, the spatial momentum for the twist $v = (0, 0, 1,\ 0, 0, 0)$ (a spin of 1 rad/s about the $z$ axis through the origin, angular part first), the kinetic energy, and the rank of the matrix.

6. ◦ A free-floating base of 4 kg moves on a frictionless line, and a slider of 2 kg moves on the base along the same line. The coordinates are the base position $x_b$ and the slider position $d$ relative to the base. Write $M$ for the [floating-base dynamics](/floating-base-dynamics/), and find $\ddot x_b$, $\ddot d$ and the acceleration of the center of mass when the joint motor pushes with $\tau = 8$ N from rest.

7. ◦ For the arm of exercise 2, the Christoffel form of $C$ is $\begin{pmatrix} -s_2 \dot q_2 & -s_2(\dot q_1 + \dot q_2) \\ s_2 \dot q_1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}$ with $s_2 = \sin q_2$. At $q_2 = \pi/6$ and $\dot q = (1, 2)$ rad/s, compute $\dot M$, $C$ and $\dot M - 2C$, and check the [skew symmetry](/skew-symmetry-of-manipulator-dynamics/).

8. ◦ A uniform rod of mass 1 kg and length 1 m ($l = 0.5$ m) leans at $\theta = 60°$ to a floor with $\mu = 2$, and its lower end slides away from the center of mass. Compute the coefficient $A = 1 + 3\cos\theta(\cos\theta - \mu\sin\theta)$ of the [Painlevé paradox](/painleve-paradox/). With $\dot\theta = 0$, does any normal force $N \ge 0$ satisfy the contact law?

9. ◦ Two masses of 2 kg and 3 kg hang on the two ends of a string over a massless pulley without friction, with heights measured upward and $g = 9.81$ m/s$^2$. Apply the [Gauss principle of least constraint](/gauss-principle-of-least-constraint/) with $A = [1, 1]$ and $b = 0$. Give both accelerations and the multiplier, and say what the multiplier is physically.

10. ◦ In the [flexible joint model](/flexible-joint-model/), a link with $mgl = 19.62$ N m is held horizontal on a joint of stiffness $K = 400$ N m/rad. Give the motor position offset $q_2 - q_1$ and the motor torque. For a second joint without gravity with link inertia $I = 2$ kg m$^2$, reflected rotor inertia $J = 0.5$ kg m$^2$ and $K = 200$ N m/rad, give the vibration frequency with the motor held still and with the motor free.

11. • A planar arm with three revolute joints, links of 1 m and a point mass of 1 kg at the end of each link, lies straight along the horizontal $x$ axis ($q = 0$) in a vertical plane. Find $M(0)$ and its determinant, the torques that hold the arm still, and the accelerations $\ddot q$ from rest when all torques are zero. Explain the accelerations by what each point mass does.

12. • A planar arm with two revolute joints has $m_1 = 2$ kg, $m_2 = 1$ kg, $L_1 = 1$ m, centers of mass at $r_1 = 0.5$ m and $r_2 = 0.4$ m from the joints, and inertias $I_1 = 0.2$ and $I_2 = 0.05$ kg m$^2$ about the centers of mass. With $a_1 = I_1 + m_1 r_1^2 + m_2 L_1^2$, $a_2 = I_2 + m_2 r_2^2$ and $a_3 = m_2 L_1 r_2$, show that $\det M(q) = a_1 a_2 - a_3^2 \cos^2 q_2$. Give $M$ at $q_2 = 0$ and the smallest and largest values of $\det M$ over all $q$.

13. • For the arm of exercise 2, show that the condition number $\kappa(q_2)$ of $M$ depends on $q_2$ only through $\mathrm{tr}(M)^2/\det M$, that it increases with $\cos q_2$, and give its largest and smallest values and where they occur.

14. • An arm in a horizontal plane has a revolute joint about the vertical axis and a prismatic joint along the arm. Everything that does not slide has the inertia $I = 0.5$ kg m$^2$ about the axis, and the slider is a point mass $m = 2$ kg at the distance $r = q_2$ from the axis. Derive $M$, the Christoffel $C$ and the torques with [Euler-Lagrange manipulator dynamics](/euler-lagrange-manipulator-dynamics/), check that $\dot M - 2C$ is skew-symmetric, and evaluate $\tau$ at $r = 0.5$ m, $\dot r = 1$ m/s, $\dot\theta = 2$ rad/s, $\ddot\theta = 1$ rad/s$^2$ and $\ddot r = 3$ m/s$^2$.

15. • A robot has a torso joint 1 on a fixed base, and two arms of three revolute joints each mounted on body 1: bodies 2, 3, 4 form one arm and bodies 5, 6, 7 the other, each the child of the one before. How many entries of the $7 \times 7$ matrix $M(q)$ can be nonzero, and what is the sum of the depths that bounds the work of the [composite rigid body algorithm](/composite-rigid-body-algorithm/)? Compare with a chain of 7 joints.

16. • A pendulum has the model $\tau = \theta_1 \ddot q + \theta_2 \sin q$, linear in the parameters as in [linearity in dynamic parameters](/linearity-in-dynamic-parameters/). Three samples give $(\ddot q, \sin q, \tau) = (1, 0, 0.4)$, $(0, 1, 4.75)$ and $(2, 0.5, 3.7)$. Find the least squares estimate of [dynamic parameter identification](/dynamic-parameter-identification/) and its residuals. Then explain why samples taken along a motion with $\ddot q = 2\sin q$ at every instant cannot identify both parameters.

17. • Take the arm of exercise 2 at $q = (0, \pi/2)$, and let the task be the $y$ coordinate of the hand, so $J = [1, 0]$. Compute the task inertia $\Lambda$, the [dynamically consistent generalized inverse](/dynamically-consistent-generalized-inverse/) $\bar J$, the projector $N = I - \bar J J$, and the kinetic energies of $\dot q = \bar J$ and $\dot q = J^+$ for $\dot y = 1$ m/s. Check that every torque $N^T \tau_0$ gives no task acceleration from rest.

18. • A robot of 60 kg stands with its center of mass at $c = (0, 0, 1)$ m. Its feet touch the floor at $(0, 0.1, 0)$ and $(0, -0.1, 0)$ m with the forces $(10, 0, 300)$ and $(-10, 0, 300)$ N, and no contact torques. Use [centroidal dynamics](/centroidal-dynamics/) to find $\dot l$, $\dot k$ and $\ddot c$, with $g = 9.81$ m/s$^2$.

19. • For the sliding rod of exercise 8 with $\mu = 1.5$, find the range of angles $\theta$ where $A < 0$. Then, for $\mu = 2$ and $\theta = 60°$ with $\dot\theta = 7$ rad/s, find every normal force that satisfies the contact law, using $b = -g + l\sin\theta\,\dot\theta^2$.

20. • A rigid bar of 2 kg and length 2 m rests horizontally on two frictionless supports at its ends $x = \pm 1$ m, with $I = 2/3$ kg m$^2$ about its center, and an upward force $P$ pulls its right end. Use the coordinates $(y, \theta)$ of the center and set up the LCP of [rigid contact complementarity](/rigid-contact-complementarity/) at $\theta = 0$. Solve it for $P = 5$ N and $P = 15$ N, and find the value of $P$ at which the right end starts to lift.

21. • A particle of 1 kg hits the floor with the velocity $v = (1, -2)$ m/s, so $\phi = 0$ at the start of the step. Take one step of [Stewart-Trinkle time stepping](/stewart-trinkle-time-stepping/) with $h = 0.01$ s, $g = 9.81$ m/s$^2$ and the two friction edges $\pm x$. Find $c_n$, the net friction impulse, $\lambda$ and $v^+$ for $\mu = 0.5$ and for $\mu = 0.2$. Is $\beta$ unique when $\mu = 0.5$?

22. • A point mass of 1 kg moves on a circle of radius 1 m about the origin, held by a massless rod, under gravity $(0, -9.81)$ m/s$^2$. At the position $(1, 0)$ its velocity is $(0, 2)$ m/s. Write the circle constraint at the level of accelerations as $Ax = b$, use the formula of Udwadia and Kalaba to find the acceleration, and give the force in the rod.

23. ★ Number the bodies of a kinematic tree so that each parent comes before its children, $\lambda(i) < i$. Prove that the factorization $M = L^T L$ with $L$ lower triangular, computed from the last row up, creates no fill-in: $L$ is nonzero only where the lower triangle of $M$ can be. Count the nonzeros of $L$ for the robot of exercise 15, and count the nonzeros of the ordinary Cholesky factor $M = L L^T$ computed from the first row down.

24. ★ For the arm of exercise 14, model the links as general planar bodies: link 1 has the parameters $(m_1, mx_1, my_1, I_1)$ and link 2 has $(m_2, mx_2, my_2, I_2)$, with first moments in each link frame and $I_i$ about the frame origin, and frame 2 at the distance $q_2$ along link 1 with axes parallel to link 1. Show that the kinetic energy is $\tfrac12 (I_1 + I_2 + m_2 q_2^2 + 2 mx_2 q_2)\dot\theta^2 + \tfrac12 m_2 \dot q_2^2 - my_2 \dot q_2 \dot\theta$, and prove that $(I_1 + I_2, m_2, mx_2, my_2)$ is a set of [base inertial parameters](/base-inertial-parameters/).

25. ★ In the reduced flexible joint model, prove that $u$ first appears in the fourth time derivative of the link position $q_1$, and find the matrix that multiplies $u$ there. Give its value for the single joint of exercise 10 with $I = 2$, $J = 0.5$ and $K = 200$.

26. ★ Body 1 is massless. Body 2 is a point mass of 1 kg at $(1, 0, 0)$ m in its own frame, attached to body 1 by a revolute joint about the $z$ axis through the origin of frame 1, at $q_2 = 0$. Compute the [articulated-body inertia](/articulated-body-inertia/) $I^A_1$ with spatial vectors ordered $(\omega_x, \omega_y, \omega_z, v_x, v_y, v_z)$, and its rank. Explain each nonzero entry, check $I^a_2 S_2 = 0$, and find the accelerations of body 1 that need no wrench at all.

27. ★ Two rigid bodies move in a plane without contact or gravity, joined by a revolute joint with the angle $\varphi$, and their total angular momentum is zero. Let $\theta$ be the orientation of body 1. Prove that after any motion in which $\varphi$ returns to its initial value, $\theta$ returns to its initial value too, and say why the same argument fails for three bodies joined by two joints. Relate the answer to the property of the [centroidal momentum matrix](/centroidal-momentum-matrix/) that its angular rows are not integrable.
