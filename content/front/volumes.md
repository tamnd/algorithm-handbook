The handbook has one volume for each of the 31 areas of the site. The table below lists them with their sizes in this edition. A volume *in this edition* has written entries and is built as a book; a *forthcoming* volume has only short pages on the site so far, and its chapters are listed here so that you can see what it will hold. The further topics of a volume are the topics of its area whose entries are not yet written.

The figure after the table shows how the built volumes depend on each other. Most arrows start at Volume 1, *Foundations*, and Volume 3, *Data structures*: nearly every other volume uses their entries.

After the figure comes one section for each volume: the summary of its area, its state, a few entries to start with, and the table of its chapters.
