- **New in this edition: Volume 37, Robotics**, with all eight of its chapters written: *Rigid body motion* (29
  entries: rotation groups, Euler angles and gimbal lock, quaternions, homogeneous transformations, twists, screws
  and wrenches, the exponential and logarithm maps), *Robot kinematics* (33 entries: kinematic chains and the
  Denavit-Hartenberg convention, forward and inverse kinematics, Jacobians, singularities and redundancy,
  manipulability and the product of exponentials), *Robot dynamics* (28 entries: the Newton-Euler and
  Euler-Lagrange equations, the recursive Newton-Euler, composite rigid body and articulated body algorithms,
  spatial vector algebra, centroidal dynamics and the Painlevé paradox), *Trajectory generation and optimization*
  (25 entries: polynomial and trapezoidal profiles, time-optimal path parameterization, minimum-jerk and
  minimum-snap trajectories, CHOMP, STOMP and differential dynamic programming), *Localization and SLAM* (54
  entries: Bayes and histogram filters, Monte Carlo localization, information and error-state Kalman filters, motion
  and sensor models, occupancy grids, EKF-SLAM,
  pose-graph optimization and loop closure), *Grasping and manipulation* (34 entries: friction cones, form and force
  closure, grasp quality, impedance, admittance and hybrid force control, visual servoing and quasi-static pushing),
  *Mobile and legged robots* (37 entries: unicycle, differential-drive and bicycle models, nonholonomic constraints,
  pure pursuit and the Stanley controller, Dubins and Reeds-Shepp paths, the zero-moment point, capture point and
  inverted pendulum models) and *Multi-robot systems* (24 entries: consensus and formation control, task allocation
  by markets and the contract net protocol, coverage control, reciprocal collision avoidance and prioritized and
  coupled planning).
- **New in this edition: Volume 38, Computer vision**, with all eight of its chapters written: *Local features and
  matching* (31 entries: corner and blob detectors, scale space, SURF, ORB and other descriptors, the ratio
  test), *Camera models and calibration* (22 entries: the pinhole camera, distortion and fisheye models, Zhang's
  and Tsai's methods, self-calibration and the Kruppa equations), *Projective and multiple view geometry* (46
  entries: homographies, epipolar geometry, the fundamental and essential matrices, the eight-point algorithm,
  perspective-n-point, triangulation and RANSAC), *Structure from motion and visual odometry* (28 entries: bundle
  adjustment, Gauss-Newton and Levenberg-Marquardt, factorization methods, rotation and translation averaging),
  *Stereo and dense reconstruction* (38 entries: rectification, matching costs, semi-global matching, graph cuts,
  multi-view stereo, photometric stereo and depth fusion), *3D registration and point clouds* (27 entries: ICP and
  its variants, normal estimation, point feature histograms, coherent point drift, NDT and global registration),
  *Segmentation and recognition* (54 entries: graph-cut segmentation and the Mumford-Shah functional, detection metrics and
  non-maximum suppression, bag of visual words, active shape and deformable part models, and matting) and *Tracking* (31 entries:
  background subtraction, the KLT tracker, mean shift, correlation filters, data association, multiple hypothesis
  tracking and the CLEAR MOT metrics).
- **Volume 39, Complexity**, now has all seven of its chapters written: to *NP completeness* (20 entries) the site
  added *Complexity classes* (63 entries: P, NP, PSPACE, EXPTIME, logspace, randomized and quantum classes, the
  hierarchy theorems, interactive proofs and circuits), *Reductions catalogue* (23 entries: Karp, Turing, Levin and
  parsimonious reductions, gadgets, Karp's 21 problems and search-to-decision), *Parameterized complexity* (64
  entries: FPT, kernels, bounded search trees, the W-hierarchy and kernel lower bounds), *Fine grained
  complexity* (31 entries: 3SUM, APSP, orthogonal vectors and SETH, and the conditional lower bounds they give),
  *Communication and query complexity* (47 entries: deterministic, randomized and nondeterministic protocols,
  fooling sets, the rank and discrepancy methods, decision trees, sensitivity and certificates) and *Computability* (36
  entries: decidability, the halting problem, Rice's theorem, Post's correspondence problem, recursive functions,
  the arithmetical hierarchy and Kolmogorov complexity). It has 284 entries.
- **New in this edition: Volume 40, Databases**, with seven of its nine chapters written: *Relational model and
  theory* (53 entries: relational algebra and calculus, functional and multivalued dependencies, normal forms and
  decomposition, conjunctive queries, Datalog and provenance), *Storage and indexing* (9 entries), *Query
  processing* (25 entries: join algorithms from nested loops to Grace and hybrid hash join, Yannakakis's
  algorithm, the AGM bound and worst-case optimal joins, top-k queries), *Query optimization* (10 entries),
  *Transactions and concurrency control* (18 entries: serializability, two-phase locking, timestamp ordering,
  multiversion and snapshot isolation), *Logging and recovery* (7 entries: write-ahead logging and ARIES) and
  *Stream and incremental processing* (4 entries: view maintenance). The database algorithms that were chapter
  26.5 in 0.36.0 are now in this volume. Chapters 40.7 and 40.8 have their openings.
- **New in this edition: Volumes 42, 43, 45 and 46** (Algorithmic game theory, Scientific computing, Security and
  privacy, Imaging and inverse problems), each with its introduction, the chapters that have entries, and the
  openings of the others. Their few entries came from other volumes: Nash equilibria and the minimax theorem from
  Volume 29, the Borda count and Kemeny rank aggregation from Volume 35, molecular dynamics from Volume 27, lattice
  Boltzmann and smoothed particle hydrodynamics from Volume 12, noninterference from Volume 34, timing attacks from
  Volume 23 and phase retrieval from Volume 28.
- **Volume 27, Biology**, now has all ten of its chapters written: six new chapters, *Read mapping and variant
  calling* (26 entries), *Population genetics* (48), *Gene finding and motifs* (31), *Gene expression and
  transcriptomics* (38), *Systems biology* (36) and *Epidemic and ecological models* (36), bring it to 468 entries.
- **Volume 28, Graphics and signals**, has six new chapters on animation and simulation: *Keyframing and
  deformation* (20 entries), *Rigid body simulation* (23) and *Fluid animation* (24) are written, and *Real-time
  rendering*, *Character animation* and *Deformable body simulation* have their openings. Volume 8, *Strings*,
  has the opening of a new chapter, *Text processing and typesetting*.
- **Volume 0, Guide and Index**, covers the whole set of 11,087 entries. It has four new learning paths: *Robotics*,
  *Computer vision*, *Complexity* and *Databases*.
- In Volumes 12, 23, 26, 27, 28, 29, 34 and 35, which lost entries to the new volumes, the entries after them have
  new numbers. The other volumes have the same entries under the same numbers as in 0.36.0. The entries have the
  corrections that the site made up to its release v0.40.0.
- Volumes 41 and 44 (Natural language and speech, Communications) are not built: the site has only short pages
  for them so far. Volume 0 lists their chapters.

**Files.** For each volume `NN-area`: `handbook-of-algorithms-0.40.0-NN-area.pdf`, `.epub`, and `-tex.tar.gz`
(`main.tex`, `body.tex`, `handbook.sty` and the figures; compile `main.tex` with XeLaTeX or tectonic).
`…-all-pdf.zip` and `…-all-epub.zip` hold every volume; `SHA256SUMS` lists the checksums.

Entry and statement numbers (such as 4.1.12 and Lemma 4.1.12.2) belong to this edition. The entries are the text of
the site; the learner pages were drafted by agents and checked as described in `content/PROVENANCE.md`. Text
